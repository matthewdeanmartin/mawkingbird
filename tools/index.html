Tools

Free tools for Mastodon and Bluesky

Small, single-purpose tools for the jobs the standard apps leave out: picking a handle, finding your friends, scheduling posts, and tidying up your account. Each one runs in your browser.

Start on Mastodon

  • Mastodon handle generator: find a free usernameGenerate Mastodon handles from your name and check which are free on the server you want and on the biggest Mastodon servers. No account needed.

Your languages

  • Mastodon in English, without the noiseSet Mastodon up for the languages you read: hide posts in others, filter trends, translate the ones you are learning, and give your posts the right language. Free.
  • Learn English with MastodonLearn English from real posts on Mastodon: English stays in your feed with a translation underneath, automatically or on request, plus people to follow. Free.

Search

  • Mastodon search: find posts, people and hashtagsSearch Mastodon for posts, people and hashtags with Twitter-style filters: from:, has:media, dates and phrases. No account needed to start.
  • Bluesky search: find posts and peopleSearch Bluesky for people and posts with Twitter-style filters: from:, language, dates and phrases. Sign in with Bluesky to search posts.
  • Similar hashtags on MastodonFind the hashtags Mastodon users put alongside yours, ranked by how often they share recent posts. Compare several at once, like #dog and #dogs. No account needed.

Find your people

  • Import your Twitter follows to MastodonFind the people you followed on Twitter who are now on Mastodon and follow them again. Your archive is read in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
  • Find your GitHub friends on MastodonFind the people you follow on GitHub who are on Mastodon, using the links in their profiles first, and follow them in a click.
  • Find your Bluesky follows on MastodonFind the people you follow on Bluesky who are also on Mastodon, from the handles in their bios first, and follow them there in a click.
  • Find your Mastodon follows on BlueskyFind the people you follow on Mastodon who are also on Bluesky, from the handles in their bios first, and follow them there in a click.
  • Find your phone contacts on MastodonFind the people in your phone contacts or Google Contacts who are on Mastodon. Your contacts stay in your browser; you follow each match yourself.
  • Find the RSS feeds of everyone you follow on MastodonTurn the websites in your Mastodon follows' profiles into one OPML file of their blogs and RSS feeds, for any feed reader. A Mawkingbird Plus feature.

Post

  • Post to Mastodon and Bluesky at the same timeWrite once and post to Mastodon and Bluesky together. Sign in with either account, connect the other, and try it from a simple composer in your browser.
  • Schedule Mastodon posts in advanceSchedule Mastodon posts in advance: write several at once, space them out daily or hourly, and your Mastodon server publishes them on time with the tab closed.

Look after your account

  • Mastodon analytics for your accountFree analytics for your Mastodon account: top posts, engagement per post, estimated reach, when you post, who you talk to most and how many followers are active.
  • Turn off boosts on Mastodon for everyone you followHide boosts from everyone you follow on Mastodon at once, without unfollowing anyone. See how many change first, and turn them back on any time.
  • Mute Amnesty and Block Amnesty: unmute and unblock everyone on MastodonUnmute or unblock every account on Mastodon at once with Mute Amnesty and Block Amnesty. See the count and back up your list first.
  • How to be (pseudo)anonymous on MastodonMake your Mastodon account hard to link to you: hide your follows, leave search and suggestions, and check what gives you away, in one step.
  • How to turn off content warnings on MastodonAuto-expand content warnings and show sensitive media on Mastodon, for everyone or only the people you trust. See your server's own setting too.
  • Export your Mastodon bookmarks to your browserDownload your Mastodon bookmarks as a bookmark file for your browser, Raindrop.io or Pinboard, and unwrap them to the links the posts shared.
  • Download images from a Mastodon accountSee every picture a Mastodon account posted on one wall, then download them all as a zip or separate files, with GIFs, videos and audio. No account needed.
