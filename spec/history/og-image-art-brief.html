Artist work request: nine Mawkingbird images

Please hand draw nine finished cards for Mawkingbird search and link previews. Each card introduces a different part of the website. The pictures below are the temporary cards currently in use; they show the present wording and format, not the artwork you need to copy.

What to deliver

  • Nine finished PNG cards, one for each filename below, each 1200 × 630 pixels and under 200 KB.
  • Use a cohesive hand-drawn style that feels like Mawkingbird. Make each topic distinct and easy to understand at thumbnail size. Mawkingbird already has a hand-drawn bird; you do not need to draw a new bird.
  • Keep the main subject and essential words within the center 630 × 630 pixels; some previews crop the left and right sides.
  • Use the current headline and supporting line shown on each card. Keep words large and legible. The blue stripe, icons and layout are examples, not required design elements.

Deliver as: default.png

Draw: One welcoming place where Mastodon, Bluesky and RSS feeds come together. Use the existing Mawkingbird bird artwork if a bird belongs in the composition.

Words: “Mastodon, Bluesky and RSS in one place” / “A free browser client for all your feeds.”
