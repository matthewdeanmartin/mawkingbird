What happens when you sign up

  1. Bluesky asks for your email address, a password and your date of birth.
  2. Choose a handle. It’s the name people mention you by.
  3. Prove you’re a person, if Bluesky asks.
  4. Approve Mawkingbird’s access, and you’re back here, signed in.

Mawkingbird never sees your password: you type it on Bluesky’s own page. When Bluesky is busy, it may queue new sign-ups for a short while; once yours is ready, come back and sign in.

After you sign up

  • Add a picture and a bio that says what you’ll post about.
  • Follow people Bluesky suggests, so your Home feed isn’t empty.
  • Write a first post that introduces you, in your own words.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Bluesky is free.

Can I use the official Bluesky app too?

Yes. One account works in every Bluesky app, Mawkingbird included.

Can I use my own domain as my handle?

Yes. You can switch to a domain you own later, in Bluesky’s settings.

Can I follow Mastodon people as well?

Yes. Mawkingbird reads both networks. You can also create a Mastodon account.
