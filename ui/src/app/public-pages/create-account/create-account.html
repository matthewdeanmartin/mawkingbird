Create a Mastodon account

Pick a server, then sign up on it with an email address and a password. It’s free. Your handle becomes @name@server, and you can follow and talk to people on every other Mastodon server from it.

Choose a server

Start on a big, general server in your language. They’re well run, it’s easy to find people from them, and they aren’t going anywhere. Small and topic servers are great for a second account.

What happens when you sign up

  1. Choose a username. It’s the first half of your handle.
  2. Enter your email address and a password, and accept the server’s rules.
  3. Open the confirmation email and follow its link.
  4. Some servers review each new account first. If yours does, you’ll get an email when it’s ready.

Servers running Mastodon 5 send you straight back here, signed in, and we help you set up. On older servers, come back afterwards and sign in.

After you sign up

  • Add a picture and a bio that says what you’ll post about.
  • Follow people who post about things you like, so your Home feed isn’t empty.
  • Write a first post that introduces you, in your own words.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Mastodon is free. Many servers ask for donations to cover their running costs.

Do I need a phone number?

No. Mastodon signs you up with an email address.

Can I use the official Mastodon app, or another app?

Yes. One account works in every Mastodon app, Mawkingbird included.

Can I have more than one account?

Yes, on the same server or on different ones. Mawkingbird can sign in to several.
