Twitter to Mastodon

Import your Twitter follows to Mastodon

Find the people you followed on Twitter who are already on Mastodon, and follow them again in a few clicks. The archive files are read in this browser, not uploaded in full. After you sign in, searches for handles found in them go to your Mastodon server.

  1. Download your Twitter archive

    On Twitter, open Settings → Your account → Download an archive of your data. It can take a day or more to arrive. There is no need to unzip it.

  2. Drop it here

    Mawkingbird opens only the few files it needs, on your device, and lists the people you followed, replied to and mentioned, closest first.

  3. Follow them on Mastodon

    No account yet? See which servers your people chose; signing up brings you straight back. Every follow is a button you press.

Questions

Is my archive uploaded anywhere?

The archive files stay in your browser. After you sign in, this page sends searches for handles found in them to your Mastodon server.

Does it follow anyone automatically?

No. A matching username is a likely match, not proof, so every follow is a button you press. You can also download the list as a CSV and keep it.
