Questions

How do I do it on the Mastodon website?

Open Preferences on your server, turn on "Always expand posts marked with content warnings", and set "Media display" to "Show all". Those settings belong to your server and are followed by its website and some apps.

Why can't Mawkingbird change my server's setting?

Mastodon lets apps read those preferences but not change them, so Mawkingbird has switches of its own. Once you are signed in, this page shows what your server's are set to.

Will I still know a post had a warning?

Yes. The warning text stays above the post; only the click to open it goes away.

Does this change what other people see?

No. It only changes how posts open for you. Authors' warnings stay on their posts for everyone else.
