Starter kits

Find your people on Mastodon

A new Mastodon account starts with an empty timeline. Each starter kit gathers one interest: the hashtags people actually use, the people worth following, and the servers where the community meets. You can read all of it before you make an account.

Every kit is written for its readers' language, and offers new local hashtags beside the busy English ones, so smaller language communities can grow their own.
