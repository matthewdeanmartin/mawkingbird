Starter kits

Who to follow on Mastodon

Mastodon shows you posts from the people you follow, in order, without an algorithm filling the gaps. That makes a brand-new account quiet. A starter kit is a short, hand-picked list of people around one interest, so your Home feed has something to read on day one.

Everyone listed asked their own server to make them discoverable. You can try a kit before you have an account: Mawkingbird keeps those follows in this browser.
