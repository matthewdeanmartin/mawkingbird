Core Mastodon features: a feed that feels like yours

Read your home timeline, public timelines and hashtag feeds. Reply, boost, favourite and bookmark posts, and keep up with conversations and notifications.

  • Organize feeds with lists, saved searches, tags and collections.
  • Discover people through starter collections and search.
  • Switch between connected accounts and bring Bluesky into the same client.
  • Browse anonymously before choosing an account; feeds end in pages rather than an endless scroll.
Explore feeds

Readability features: from headlines to long reads

Follow RSS feeds alongside social posts, keep a reading list with bookmarks, and open supported content in a focused reader view.

  • Read RSS as headlines or articles, with read and unread tracking.
  • Add feeds and import or export subscriptions with OPML.
  • Save bookmarks and revisit them at the end of your feed.
  • Fetch supported linked pages and format their content for reading. Site access and proxy availability can affect article extraction.
Read RSS

Creators: space to write and keep your drafts

Compose social posts, keep unfinished ideas in drafts, and use the writing workspace for longer work when enabled in your deployment.

  • Return to saved drafts instead of starting over.
  • Use a focused writing workspace and publication tools.
  • Connect supported publishing services such as Blogger and Hugo.
  • Use optional OpenRouter tools with your own configured connection.
See your drafts

Advanced user features: more control, fewer repetitive steps

Manage accounts, use bulk actions, connect third-party APIs and diagnose connection problems from the same client.

  • Manage follows, mutes and blocks, including supported bulk account operations.
  • Configure third-party connections and publishing integrations.
  • Customize reading, appearance and account settings.
  • Use connection help and diagnostic tools when a server or network is unreachable.
