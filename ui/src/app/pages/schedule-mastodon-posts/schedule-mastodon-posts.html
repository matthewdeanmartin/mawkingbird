Questions

Can Mastodon schedule posts?

Yes. Every Mastodon server can hold a post and publish it later, but the standard Mastodon website has no button for it. This page uses that built-in scheduling, so the posts are kept by your own server, not by Mawkingbird.

Do I need to keep this page open?

No. Once a post is scheduled it lives on your Mastodon server, which publishes it at the chosen time whether or not your computer is on.

Are there limits?

Mastodon allows 300 scheduled posts waiting at once and 25 on any one day, and each must be at least five minutes away. The scheduler checks these before it sends anything.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. Your scheduled posts are listed under the scheduler, and any of them can be cancelled until it goes out.

Does this work for Bluesky?

Not here. Bluesky has no built-in scheduling, so this page is for Mastodon only.
