Questions

How do I download all the images from a Mastodon account?

Paste the account above, press Download media, and choose a zip. Mastodon itself only lets you save pictures one at a time, or download an archive of your own account.

Can I download my own Mastodon media?

Yes: paste your own handle. For a full backup of your account, posts included, use your server's own data export too (Preferences → Import and export).

Why couldn't some files be downloaded?

A web page can only save a file when the server holding it allows other websites to read it. Most Mastodon servers do; some don't. Those files are listed as links, so you can open and save each one yourself.

Are the files sent through Mawkingbird?

No. Your browser fetches them from the Mastodon servers and makes the zip itself. Nothing is uploaded.

Is it OK to download someone's pictures?

Their pictures still belong to them. Keeping a copy for yourself is one thing; posting them elsewhere needs their permission.
