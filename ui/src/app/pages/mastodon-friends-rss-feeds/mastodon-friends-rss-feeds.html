Questions

Why is this a Plus feature?

Checking hundreds of strangers' websites goes through a proxy that Mawkingbird pays for, one request per site. Plus covers that cost.

What is an OPML file?

The subscription-list format every feed reader understands. Import it into Feedly, NetNewsWire, Inoreader or any other reader to follow all the feeds at once.

Will it find everyone?

Only people who link a website from their profile, and only sites that publish a feed. Big platforms that never carry a personal feed are skipped without being checked.

Does a second run cost as much?

No. Sites already checked are remembered, so after following a few new people a fresh scan only checks their sites.
