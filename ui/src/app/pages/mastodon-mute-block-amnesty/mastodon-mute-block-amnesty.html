Questions

What does Mute Amnesty change?

Every account you muted is unmuted, so their posts and notifications reach you again. Muted words and filters stay exactly as they are.

What does Block Amnesty change?

Every account you blocked is unblocked. They can follow you, see your posts and reply again. Nobody is told that you blocked them, or that you stopped.

Can I undo an amnesty?

Only from a backup. Download your list when the review offers it, and you can import it again later from your Mastodon server's import page.

Does it touch domain blocks?

No. Only accounts you muted or blocked one by one are changed.
