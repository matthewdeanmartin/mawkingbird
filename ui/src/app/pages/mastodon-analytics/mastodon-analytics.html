Questions

Where is my data sent?

Nowhere new. Your posts and followers are read from your own Mastodon server and the numbers are calculated in this browser. Mawkingbird keeps no copy.

Why is reach an estimate?

Mastodon does not count views. Reach is estimated from your followers and how they interact, and the page says so next to the number.
