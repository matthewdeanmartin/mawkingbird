Questions

Is the Bluesky copy exactly the same?

The text is. The Bluesky copy is always public, and polls and content warnings stay on the Mastodon copy, because Bluesky has no equivalent.

Where are my sign-ins kept?

In this browser. Mawkingbird talks to your Mastodon server and to Bluesky directly; there is no Mawkingbird server in between holding your accounts.
