Questions

Anonymous or pseudonymous?

Pseudonymous: people see a name you made up, not your real one, and the account is hard to link to you. Truly anonymous is harder; see the next answer.

Can my server's admins see who I am?

They can see the email address you signed up with and the IP addresses you connect from. No setting changes that. Choose a server you trust, and an email address that doesn't carry your name.

Are my posts private after this?

No. Public posts stay public. These settings make your account harder to find and harder to tie to you; who can read each post is still set when you write it.

Does Mawkingbird add anything?

Yes: private follows and likes that nobody is told about, private lists, links cleaned of trackers, and photos stripped of location details, all kept in your browser.
