Questions

Does this unfollow anyone?

No. You keep following everyone, and their own posts and replies still reach your home timeline. Only their boosts stop.

Can I undo it?

Yes. The same page turns boosts back on for everyone, and you can switch them on for a single account from that person's profile.

What about people I follow later?

New follows start with boosts on, as Mastodon always does. Run this again whenever you like; it only changes the accounts that need it.

Why does it take a while?

Mastodon has no "everyone at once" setting, so each account you follow is changed one by one. A long follow list takes a few minutes.
