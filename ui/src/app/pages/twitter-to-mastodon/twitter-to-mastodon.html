Twitter to Mastodon

Import your Twitter follows to Mastodon

Find the people you followed on Twitter who are already on Mastodon, and follow them again in a few clicks. Your archive is read in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

  1. Download your Twitter archive

    On Twitter or X, open Settings → Your account → Download an archive of your data. It can take a day or more to arrive. Unzip it when it does.

  2. Choose the archive here

    Pick the unzipped folder, or just its data/following.js and data/tweets.js files. Mawkingbird counts the people you followed, replied to and mentioned.

  3. Sign in with Mastodon and follow

    Mawkingbird searches your Mastodon server for each handle and shows likely matches with the evidence for each one, so you decide who to follow.

Questions

Is my archive uploaded anywhere?

No. The files are read by this page in your browser. Only the handle searches go to your own Mastodon server, and only after you sign in.

Does it follow anyone automatically?

No. A matching username is a likely match, not proof, so every follow is a button you press. You can also download the list as a CSV and keep it.
