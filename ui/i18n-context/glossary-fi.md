# Finnish (`fi`) terminology

Use contemporary Finnish with informal singular address. Second-person verbs
and possessive suffixes often suffice on their own. Use short imperatives for
actions and sentence case for labels. Use Finnish quotation marks (”…”).
Preserve interpolation names, markup, product names, and literal examples.

In counted labels, use the singular partitive for the plural case, as in
`{{count}} julkaisua`. In mixed fragments or around arbitrary external names,
prefer word order that leaves placeholders intact. Preserve external field
names readers must locate in product setup, including GitHub's
`Permissions → Repository`, Google Cloud's `Authorized redirect URIs`, and
Raindrop.io's `Test token`.

The moderation phrases **eroottinen hieronta** and **seksipalvelut** are
idiomatic and specific. **Eroottinen hieronta ajanvarauksella** reads as an ad.
**Urheiluhieronta juoksun jälkeen** reads as ordinary text.
