# Hungarian (`hu`) terminology

Use contemporary Hungarian with informal singular address (te), direct imperatives,
and Hungarian accents. Omit pronouns in short controls. Preserve account handles,
URL examples, technical identifiers, placeholders, markup, emoji and product names.
Hungarian uses the singular noun after a number, so numeric one/other forms can
naturally share wording while their separate source keys remain intact.

Boost means re-sharing a social post, never signal amplification. A thread is a
conversation, not sewing thread. Keep Calm, Algo, Lite, Plus and other feature
brands unchanged. The fail-whale joke is adapted as Hibabálna within its short
footer budget. Account follow policy Open is Nyitott, whereas an Open button is
Megnyitás. A network probe's Control is Kontroll, not interface control.

Preserve external configuration labels that readers must locate verbatim, such as
`gist`, `Web application`, `Only select repositories` and `Permissions → Repository`.
A clipboard Paste action is Beillesztés; the publishing action explicitly says
Szövegrészlet közzététele. Thread muting controls distinguish notifications from
blocking the author. Authentication tokens and AI token counts retain token with
their context stated by the surrounding label.

This initial pass covers exactly 2,758 selected keys, including required Lite and
offline copy and short login/search strings. Long editorial prose is deferred to
English fallback. The complete dictionary was validated through the merge checker
for placeholders, markup and declared length limits, and repeated English values
were reviewed for contextual differences. Native-speaker review and complete
narrow/wide signed-in walkthroughs remain necessary before production promotion.
