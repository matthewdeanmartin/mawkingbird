War in Ukraine on Mastodon

Follow the war in Ukraine through reporting, analysis and accounts of its impact on civilians. Find people sharing sustained coverage of the invasion, humanitarian needs, human rights and reconstruction.

Choose accounts and hashtags, then preview their public posts to find your mix. Relevant tags remain useful even when quiet. War coverage can include distressing images, and open hashtag feeds can contain misleading claims: check sources and attribution before sharing.

Questions

Do I need an account?

You can browse and preview public posts without an account. Sign in with your Mastodon account to follow people and hashtags or create a list.

What coverage belongs here?

Regular reporting, sourced analysis, civilian experiences, humanitarian relief and documentation of the war. The selection excludes Russian state-controlled media and promotion of pro-invasion propaganda. Independent Russian journalism can belong here. Inclusion does not verify every claim, and public hashtag feeds are not curated.

How do I get onto this list?

Post regularly and substantively about the war and allow your server to suggest your profile. A flag or location alone is not enough; recent posts need to show a sustained focus. For help or removal, contact @mawkingbird@mastodon.social.

How we choose

People are listed when they asked their server to make them discoverable, or when they are public figures with public evidence: a Wikipedia or Wikidata entry, a YouTube channel with 10,000 subscribers, or 2,500 followers on Mastodon.

An account that looks like it wants to stay out of view is left out: few followers, hidden follower lists, no profile picture, together with a request not to be indexed. Anyone who turns off discoverability is never listed.

A language model checks that each person really posts about the topic, quoting their own posts as evidence. Every account is checked again each month: quiet accounts drop off and come back when they post again.

Need help?

Questions, or listed here and would rather not be? Message us on Mastodon and you will be removed for good. @mawkingbird@mastodon.social
