Questions

Do I need a YouTube account?
No. Mawkingbird reads each channel’s public feed, so new videos show up in your subscriptions without signing in to YouTube.
Who is listed?
Creators whose YouTube channel and Mastodon account point to each other, through their profile, their channel or Wikidata, and who posted or uploaded recently. Some entries are accounts that repost a channel’s videos; they are marked as video feed accounts.
How do I get listed or removed?
The kits are refreshed regularly from public profiles. If you would rather not be listed, contact Mawkingbird and you will be left out of future refreshes.
