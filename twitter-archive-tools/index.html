Twitter archive

Twitter archive tools

Got the .zip and not sure what to do with it? Open it here, no unzipping: search your tweets and likes, see your Twitter years in numbers, and keep it as a website. The full archive is not uploaded. If you use Find your people, handle searches go to your Mastodon server after sign-in.

Loading the archive tools…

  1. Search your tweets

    Every tweet, thread and like, searchable by word, phrase, year or type. Find the one you half remember.

  2. See your numbers

    Your years on Twitter, your most-liked posts, who you talked to most and when you posted. For you only, not a share card.

  3. Keep it as a website

    Download a small website of your own posts, a page per year with an RSS feed, to put on any host or keep in a folder.

Questions

Is my archive uploaded anywhere?

No. Your browser opens the .zip and reads only the files these tools need: your follows, tweets, likes and profile. Direct messages, email, phone number and logs are never opened.

Where are my people now?

Many moved to Mastodon. Find your Twitter people on Mastodon with the same archive.
