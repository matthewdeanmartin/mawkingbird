Questions

Doesn't Mastodon export bookmarks already?

Its data export lists your bookmarks as a spreadsheet of post addresses, which browsers and bookmark services don't import. This file they do, with titles, text and hashtags.

What does "unwrap" mean?

Most bookmarked posts were shared for a link: an article, a video, a project. Unwrapping saves that link instead of the post about it, with the post's text kept as the description.

Are my bookmarks uploaded anywhere?

No. They are read from your Mastodon server and the file is made in your browser. Nothing is sent to Mawkingbird.

Are the dates right?

Each bookmark is dated by when the post was written. Mastodon doesn't record when you bookmarked it.
