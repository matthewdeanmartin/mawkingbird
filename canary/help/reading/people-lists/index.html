Reading

Sort the people you follow into lists#

Mawkingbird can sort everyone you follow into eleven private Mastodon lists, such as Top friends, Chatty and Broadcasters. Each list name starts with “Mawkingbird:”, and a person can be in more than one. Open a list to read just those people.

Who ends up in a list#

  • Only people you follow. Followers you don't follow aren't included.
  • Nobody is followed, unfollowed, muted or notified.
  • Sorting looks at your whole follow list and your recent notifications, posts and Home feed. People who rarely post may not land in Chatty or Broadcasters.

Keeping lists fresh#

The lists are a snapshot. Mawkingbird offers a refresh after a week, when your follow count changes, or when a list goes missing. You can update any time.

The job keeps running if you close the dialog or go to another page. Reloading or switching accounts stops it; run Update again to finish.

What an update changes#

An update adds people who match and removes people who don't, including anyone you added by hand. Your other lists are never touched. To keep hand-added people, tick Only add, don't remove from lists.
