Connections and accounts

Connect a Mataroa blog#

The Mataroa connection publishes longer posts from Mawkingbird to one Mataroa blog.

Before connecting#

You need:

  • the key from the API page in your Mataroa dashboard;
  • the public address of your blog;
  • the Mawkingbird proxy, or a self-hosted proxy.

The proxy can read the Mataroa key and the posts sent through it. Continue only if you are comfortable with that.

Connect#

  1. Open Settings → Connections → Blog (Mataroa).
  2. Paste the API key.
  3. Enter the Public blog address.
  4. Decide whether to include the blog's public feed on your Mawkingbird profile.
  5. Read and select the proxy acknowledgment.
  6. Choose Save and test connection.

After a successful test, Mataroa appears as a destination in the composer.

Disconnect#

Choose Disconnect on the connection page. Revoke or replace the key in Mataroa if you no longer want the old key to publish, edit, or delete posts.
