Connections and accounts

Video feeds#

Open More → Videos, RSS → Video feeds, or Feeds → Video feeds to read YouTube channels. Paste a channel URL, @handle, UC… channel ID, or channel RSS URL and choose Subscribe. No YouTube account or Google API key is needed.

Subscriptions appear on the left. Select a channel or Main feed to read uploads. The sort menu offers Newest first, Oldest first, Title A–Z and Channel A–Z. Saved videos shows the videos you saved. The reader uses the channel's public RSS feed, which contains recent uploads rather than its entire archive. Choose Refresh to check for updates. Failed refreshes preserve cached content and never unsubscribe you.

Use Load YouTube player, then Play, to watch an embedded video. Playback is never automatic. Some videos cannot be embedded; Open on YouTube opens the original. Save videos for later or mark them watched with the card controls. Watch Party opens the existing Watch page with its conversation and temporary party hashtags. Post Response opens the mini composer with the video title, link and channel hashtag (for example, #TechnologyConnections). Add your own response, choose Mastodon, Bluesky or both from your connected posting targets, and post when ready.

In Feeds → Video feeds, you can also subscribe, exclude a channel from the reader, or unsubscribe. These subscriptions are account-scoped in this browser, separate from blog RSS subscriptions, and included in personal settings backups.

Mawkingbird Plus adds anonymous YouTube video and channel search. Search uses InnerTube through the Mawkingbird proxy; the Mawkingbird account authenticates Plus access to the proxy, while no user credentials reach YouTube. Search results can be watched or their channels subscribed to.

Public channel metadata and RSS reads use a narrow, free YouTube proxy route with per-minute limits and caching. They do not consume the general proxy's one/five-request daily sample. Search is checked for Plus access by both the client and the proxy. Turning off the RSS or CORS proxy connection stops new network reads; subscriptions and cached items remain.

Deployment requires the matching youtube and youtube-search routes in mawkingbird_cors_proxy. Neither route relays video streams or YouTube account cookies. YouTube may throttle, refuse requests or change its internal API; subscriptions remain usable through RSS when search is unavailable.
