Connections and accounts

Add, switch, or remove an account#

Mawkingbird can remember more than one Mastodon or Bluesky account in the same browser. Each account keeps its own Home feed, follows, connections, and settings.

Add another account#

  1. Open the account menu beside your profile picture.
  2. Choose + Add Mastodon account or + Add Bluesky account.
  3. Complete sign-in for that account.

Adding an account does not merge the accounts. It gives you a quick way to move between them.

Switch accounts#

  1. Open the account menu.
  2. Under Switch account, choose the account you want.

Always check the profile picture and name before posting. A draft, connection, or setting may belong only to the active account.

Log out#

Open the account menu and choose Log out. If you are browsing without an account, the button says Exit anonymous.

Logging out is different from deleting local data. An account may remain listed so you can manage the information this browser still holds.

Remove saved information#

Open Settings → Signed-in accounts. For an account that is not currently active, you can choose:

  • Delete data to remove that account's saved settings, RSS feeds, linked accounts, and local moderation information from this browser without logging it out.
  • Delete data & log out to remove that local information and its saved sign-in.
  • Delete data & leave for an anonymous session.

These actions affect this browser. They do not delete the Mastodon or Bluesky account itself.

Mawkingbird protects the active account from being partly erased while you are using it. Switch to another account first if the account you want to clean up cannot be changed.
