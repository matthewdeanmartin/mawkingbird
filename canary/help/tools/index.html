Tools

Tools#

Open the tools directory when you have a particular job in mind. Each tool explains which account or files you need.

  • Find people after a move. Find your Bluesky follows on Mastodon or find your Mastodon follows on Bluesky. If you’re starting from a Twitter archive, use the Unbird guide.
  • Publish on two networks. Open Crosspost and check the selected accounts before publishing.
  • Publish later. Open Schedule Mastodon posts and follow the scheduling guide to review the time and destination.
