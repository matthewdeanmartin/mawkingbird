Write a blog post#

Open Blog → Write with your Mastodon account selected. The Blog post panel tells you whether your draft will appear as an article.

  1. Enter a Title. It becomes the first line of your Mastodon post.
  2. Write the body. A long post or a thread can qualify as an article. For a short piece, choose the option to mark it with BLOG:.
  3. Check the message in the Blog post panel. To come back later, save the draft and reopen it from Drafts.
  4. Review the destination and visibility before publishing. Once it’s live, open Mine to read the article as your readers will see it.

If you also publish on an external blog, connect your blog to make it available under Also publish to.
