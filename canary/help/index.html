Mawkingbird Help#

Which part of Mawkingbird are you using? Choose a guide below. In the app, Apps at the end of the top menu lets you switch between activities.

New to Mastodon? Create a Mastodon account on a big server in your language.

  • Mastodon — choose a server, find people and join a conversation.
  • Bluesky — sign in with your handle, read your timeline and post to Bluesky.
  • Blog — read long Mastodon posts as articles and write your own.
  • Radio — find recordings, save singles and play a playlist.
  • Twitter to Mastodon (Unbird) — open your Twitter archive and find people you used to follow.
  • RSS — follow a website or blog in your reader.
  • Video — watch video posts and follow YouTube channels.
  • Forum — follow a conversation from its first post through the replies.
  • Tools — pick a handle, find friends across networks or schedule posts.

For settings and features used across apps, browse shared guides, connections or the glossary.
