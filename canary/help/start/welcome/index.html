Start here

Welcome to Mawkingbird#

Mawkingbird is a place to read and take part in social media. It can show posts from Mastodon, Bluesky, and other sources together, so you do not have to keep checking separate sites.

You can use Mawkingbird in two ways:

  • Look around without an account. Read public posts and learn how the app works. Choices such as who you follow are kept in this browser.
  • Sign in with an account you already have. Read your own feed, follow people, reply, like, repost, and publish posts.

What happens on your first visit#

Mawkingbird opens with a small sample of posts behind a welcome box. The sample is only a preview. It does not mean Mawkingbird chose people for you to follow.

Choose Log in if you already have a Mastodon or Bluesky account. Choose Continue without logging in if you want to explore first.

You can sign in later. Looking around does not lock you into a choice.

What you will see#

The main part of the screen is a feed: a stream of posts. Your Home feed becomes personal as you follow people and connect accounts.

The navigation also gives you places to:

  • search for people and subjects;
  • choose other feeds;
  • read replies and notifications;
  • chat;
  • write a post;
  • change your settings.

Do not worry about learning every part at once. Your first ten minutes covers the few things you need to begin.

A note about unfamiliar words#

Social networks use words such as feed, handle, server, and repost. The guides explain these words when they matter. You can also use the glossary whenever a label is unfamiliar.
