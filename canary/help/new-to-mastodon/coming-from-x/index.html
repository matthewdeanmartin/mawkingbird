New to Mastodon

Coming to Mastodon from X#

Mastodon looks like Twitter did before it became X, and Mawkingbird deliberately looks like Twitter did in 2018. Most habits carry over. These are the differences you will notice first.

The same things with different names#

On X On Mastodon
Post or tweet Post (older guides say toot)
Repost or retweet Boost
Like Favourite
Bookmark Bookmark
Direct message Private mention

Mawkingbird uses the familiar words in many places, so you will see both. The glossary lists them all.

No algorithm decides your Home feed#

Home shows the people and hashtags you follow, in order. There is no "For you" feed pushing strangers in, and posts do not disappear because they did not perform.

If you miss a feed sorted by what is popular, Mawkingbird's Algo tab sorts posts from the people and tags you follow by their replies, boosts and favourites. It never adds accounts you did not choose.

Your followers do not come with you#

Nothing moves your X followers, posts or handle to Mastodon automatically. Mawkingbird does not do it either. You rebuild your feed by following people again, and the starter kits give you a head start.

If friends have already moved, ask them for their full Mastodon handles and import them as a list.

Hashtags matter more#

Mastodon search is more limited than X search. A word search only finds posts whose authors opted in, so hashtags are how most posts get found. Add one or two relevant tags to posts you want strangers to see, and follow tags for subjects you care about.

Private mentions are not encrypted#

A private mention is only shown to the people mentioned, but it is not encrypted. Your server's administrators can technically read it. Use an encrypted messenger for anything sensitive.

Quote posts work differently#

Mastodon added quote posts later than X, and people can choose whether their posts may be quoted. Some people and servers still dislike quote posts used to mock someone. Replying is the more common habit.

Verification uses links#

There is no paid check mark. You can verify that you own a website by linking it from your profile and linking back from the site; your server then shows the link in green. Mawkingbird can also show blue checks just for fun, under Settings → Appearance, either for everyone or only for accounts with more followers than you.
