Finding people

Explore bundled Mastodon collections#

A collection is a set of Mastodon accounts chosen by another person. Bundled collections are saved copies of real collections that Mawkingbird makes easier to discover.

They differ from Mawkingbird Starter Kits: a collection belongs to its original curator, while a Starter Kit is assembled for Mawkingbird newcomers.

The bundled catalog will continue to grow. Use the titles, descriptions, curators, and member lists currently shown in Mawkingbird rather than expecting a fixed list.

Browse collections#

  1. Open More → Find Friends.
  2. Choose Bundled collections.
  3. Open a collection that sounds interesting.

The Collection tab lists its members. The Posts tab lets you request a sample of recent posts from some members. Sampling posts is a good way to judge whether the collection suits your feed before following everyone.

Follow people from a collection#

Choose Follow everyone in this collection when you want the entire set. Mawkingbird resolves and follows each account separately, then reports the result.

For a more selective approach:

  1. Open a member's profile.
  2. Read the biography and recent posts.
  3. Choose Follow only when you want that person's future posts.

Collections can become stale#

A bundled collection is a snapshot. Since it was saved, an account may have moved, changed its handle, stopped posting, or been removed by its owner. Some members may therefore fail to load or follow.

Use Open original on its home server when you want to compare Mawkingbird's snapshot with the original collection.

Following the collection follows its accounts; it does not guarantee that future changes made by the curator will automatically change your follows.
