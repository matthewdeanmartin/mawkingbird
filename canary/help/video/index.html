Video#

Choose Apps → Video when you want to watch. Open Posts for video from the social feed, or YouTube for channels you follow as feeds.

To add a channel, follow it as a video feed. Return to Video → YouTube to find its uploads. Use Queue to see what’s lined up to watch.
