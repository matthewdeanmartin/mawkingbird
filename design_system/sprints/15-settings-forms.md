# Sprint 15 — settings form composition

Status: **implemented; review ready**. Sprint 16 is next; three planned sprints
remain (16–18). See [review](../REVIEW-15.md), [audit](../audits/15-settings-forms.md)
and [progress](../PROGRESS.md).

Opening scope: adopt existing approved widgets in SettingsPrivacy and
SettingsContent's Trust controls. Preserve all four trust values, per-field
credential writes, rollback, translations, default language, named-account state
and explicit destructive confirmation. No new component variants are needed.

Delivered: 13 shared placements in the two real app pages and a real-component
preview with isolated services. Keyboard, failed saves, pending states and narrow,
dark and RTL layouts are covered. Residual account actions and initial read-state
behavior remain explicit partial adoption rather than whole-page completion.

Exit checks: targeted specs, complete app gate, production budget, catalogue
verification, screenshots and regenerated adoption/ledger reports.
