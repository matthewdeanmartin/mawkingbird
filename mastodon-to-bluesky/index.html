Mastodon to Bluesky

Find your Mastodon follows on Bluesky

Many of the people you follow on Mastodon are on Bluesky too. This finds them and lets you follow them there. Sign in with either account, connect the other, and see who is already there.

  1. Sign in to both

    Sign in with your Mastodon or your Bluesky account, then connect the other one on the next screen.

  2. Read who you follow

    Everyone you follow on Mastodon who wrote their Bluesky handle in their bio is matched straight away, at no cost.

  3. Search for the rest, then follow

    Look up the others by name, as many as you choose, then follow the ones you recognise with one button.

Questions

Will it follow people without asking?

No. People who named their other handle themselves are ticked for you; guesses from a name search are not. Nothing is followed until you press Follow.

Why are some people missing?

Only people who mention their Bluesky handle, or whose name finds them in a search, can be matched. Someone with a common name and no handle in their bio may not turn up.

Can I go the other way?

Yes: find your Bluesky follows on Mastodon.

Where are my sign-ins kept?

In this browser. Mawkingbird talks to your Mastodon server and to Bluesky directly; there is no Mawkingbird server in between holding your accounts.
