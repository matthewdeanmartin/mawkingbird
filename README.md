# Mawkingbird

**Read Mastodon, Bluesky, and RSS together.**

[Try Mawkingbird in your browser](https://mawkingbird.com/)

![Mawkingbird's Home feed in the web app](ui/readme_assets/web-preview.png)

## What you can do

- Bring Mastodon, Bluesky, and RSS into one Home feed.
- Import your twitter archive to find your friends
- Invite the rest of your friends
- Post to Mastodon and Bluesky.
- Integrated with RSS with in app article reading.
- Starter collections to find people to follow.
- Superior search
