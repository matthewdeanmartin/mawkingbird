Questions

Why can't Mastodon just search for this?

Each Mastodon server only searches the posts it holds, and many servers do not offer full-text search at all. So this page reads both accounts' latest public posts from their own servers and keeps the ones that reply to or mention the other. It says how far back it looked.

Why is Bluesky better with an account?

Bluesky's post search needs a signed-in account. Without one, this page reads both accounts' recent posts instead, which finds recent exchanges but not old ones.

Can I compare a Mastodon account with a Bluesky one?

Not directly: people on different networks cannot reply to each other without a bridge, so there is nothing to find. Use the same network for both.

Are private posts included?

No. Only public posts are read, the same ones anyone can see on the accounts' profiles.
