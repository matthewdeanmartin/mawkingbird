Find your phone contacts on Mastodon

Some of the people in your address book are already on Mastodon. This looks for them and lets you follow the ones you recognise. Sign in with Mastodon, pick your contacts or upload a contacts file, and see who turns up.

  1. Sign in with Mastodon

    The search runs through your own Mastodon server.

  2. Choose your contacts

    On an Android phone in Chrome, pick people straight from your contacts. Anywhere else, upload a contacts file exported from Google Contacts.

  3. Follow the ones you know

    Each match shows why it was suggested. Follow people one at a time; nothing is followed for you.

Questions

Are my contacts uploaded?

No. Your contacts stay in this browser. Only search terms made from them, such as a name or a username, are sent to your own Mastodon server, and nothing is saved when you close the page.

How do I get a contacts file?

In Google Contacts, choose Export and pick the Google CSV format, then upload that file here.

Why is the match a guess?

Mastodon has no "find by phone number or email" search, so people are matched on clues: a Mastodon handle saved on the contact, their name, the name and domain of their email address, their website. A distinctive name is found easily; a common one may bring up strangers, which is why you follow each person yourself.
