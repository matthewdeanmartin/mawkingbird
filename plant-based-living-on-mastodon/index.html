Questions

Do I need an account?

You can explore the recommendations and preview public posts without an account. To follow people and tags or create a Mastodon list, sign in with your Mastodon account.

What does positive mean here?

We look for helpful everyday ideas and constructive conversation. Accounts mainly sharing graphic animal suffering or using shock and shaming are outside this selection. Ethical discussion is welcome. Public tags and accounts can change, so preview your choices; this is a curated starting point, not a guarantee about every post.

How do I get onto this list?

Share useful vegan, vegetarian or plant-based posts regularly and allow your profile to be suggested in your server settings. Recommendations also depend on recent activity, topic fit and review. For help or removal, contact @mawkingbird@mastodon.social.

How we choose

People are listed when they asked their server to make them discoverable, or when they are public figures with public evidence: a Wikipedia or Wikidata entry, a YouTube channel with 10,000 subscribers, or 2,500 followers on Mastodon.

An account that looks like it wants to stay out of view is left out: few followers, hidden follower lists, no profile picture, together with a request not to be indexed. Anyone who turns off discoverability is never listed.

A language model checks that each person really posts about the topic, quoting their own posts as evidence. Every account is checked again each month: quiet accounts drop off and come back when they post again.

Need help?

Questions, or listed here and would rather not be? Message us on Mastodon and you will be removed for good. @mawkingbird@mastodon.social
