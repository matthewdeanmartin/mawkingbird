A new Mastodon account starts with an empty timeline. Finding the people who
post about what you care about is the slow part, and it is where many people
give up.

[Mastodon communities by interest](https://mawkingbird.com/communities/) is our
answer. Each page covers one interest and gathers three things:

- **Hashtags** people actually use for that interest.
- **People** worth following, from writers and YouTubers to active community
  members.
- **Servers** where the community meets.

You can read all of it, and preview the feed it would build, before you make an
account.

## The first topics

- [Science fiction & fantasy](https://mawkingbird.com/science-fiction-on-mastodon/)
- [Space & astronomy](https://mawkingbird.com/space-and-astronomy-on-mastodon/)
- [Math](https://mawkingbird.com/math-on-mastodon/)
- [Gardening & houseplants](https://mawkingbird.com/gardening-and-houseplants-on-mastodon/)
- [Plant-based living](https://mawkingbird.com/plant-based-living-on-mastodon/)
- [ESP32 & single-board computers](https://mawkingbird.com/esp32-and-single-board-computers-on-mastodon/)

## Not only in English

Pages are written for readers in their own language: German, French, Spanish,
Italian and Japanese so far, depending on the topic. Beside the busy English
hashtags, they suggest local ones, so smaller language communities can grow
their own.

## Then build your feed

When you are ready, pick the tags and people you want, sign in with any Mastodon
account, and Mawkingbird follows them for you. If a hashtag turns out to be
flooded by a few very busy accounts, the page helps you spot them and mute them
before they take over your timeline.
