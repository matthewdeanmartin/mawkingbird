Mawkingbird is a Mastodon and Bluesky client that runs in your browser. Here is
what changed between mid-October and early November.

## Listening and reading

- **Radio.** Posts that share music and audio now play as a radio station, with
  a Listen page, embeds from common audio sites and preferences for what plays.
- **Studio** for preparing audio posts and finding your audio that is ready to share.
- **Forum.** A new mode that finds the people talking to each other in a feed
  and shows each conversation as a whole discussion.
- **Slide show** for posts with several images.
- **Read later** got better.

## Finding things

- **Similar hashtags** suggest tags next to the one you're reading.
- **Mastodon communities by interest**: see
  [the launch post](posts/communities-by-interest/).
- Home timeline tuning has new options.

## Getting around

- **App modes** and a reworked, slimmer main menu.
- Layout fixes on profiles, on the home toolbar and in the account picker.
- A **retry button** when a page fails to load.
- Images are no longer lost when you edit a post.

## Bluesky

- Many fixes and improvements for Bluesky accounts across the app.

## Free tools, in more languages

- New single-purpose tools, such as downloading a post's media and saving
  bookmarks.
- Guides for people arriving from Twitter.
- Every language the app is translated into now has its own landing pages.
