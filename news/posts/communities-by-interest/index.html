Find your people: Mastodon communities by interest

November 7, 2026 · 1 min read

  • Launches
  • Communities

A new Mastodon account starts with an empty timeline. Finding the people who post about what you care about is the slow part, and it is where many people give up.

Mastodon communities by interest is our answer. Each page covers one interest and gathers three things:

  • Hashtags people actually use for that interest.
  • People worth following, from writers and YouTubers to active community members.
  • Servers where the community meets.

You can read all of it, and preview the feed it would build, before you make an account.

The first topics

  • Science fiction & fantasy
  • Space & astronomy
  • Math
  • Gardening & houseplants
  • Plant-based living
  • ESP32 & single-board computers

Not only in English

Pages are written for readers in their own language: German, French, Spanish, Italian and Japanese so far, depending on the topic. Beside the busy English hashtags, they suggest local ones, so smaller language communities can grow their own.

Then build your feed

When you are ready, pick the tags and people you want, sign in with any Mastodon account, and Mawkingbird follows them for you. If a hashtag turns out to be flooded by a few very busy accounts, the page helps you spot them and mute them before they take over your timeline.

Tell us which interest should come next by replying to this post on Mastodon.
