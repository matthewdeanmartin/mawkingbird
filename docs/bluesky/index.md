# Bluesky

[Sign in with Bluesky](../start/sign-in-bluesky.md) to read your Bluesky timeline in **Home**. Mastodon and Bluesky share the social screens under **Fedi** in the Apps menu.

## Read and reply

Open **Home** with your Bluesky account selected. Choose a post to read its replies. Use **Reply** to answer, the heart to like it, or **Repost** to share it with your followers.

## Write for Bluesky

Choose **Quick post** on Home, write your message and check that **Bluesky** is selected below the text box before choosing **Post**. If you connected both networks, [choose your destinations](../posting/destinations.md) to send a copy to each.

To add Bluesky while you're already using a Mastodon account, follow [Connect Bluesky](../connections/bluesky.md).
