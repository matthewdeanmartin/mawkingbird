# Sign in

Mawkingbird works with an account you already have. On the sign-in screen, choose the network where that account lives.

## Which choice should I use?

Choose **Mastodon** if you normally sign in through a site such as `mastodon.social`, Fosstodon, Hachyderm, or another Mastodon server.

Choose **Bluesky** if your account has a handle such as `yourname.bsky.social` or uses your own domain as its handle.

If you have both, start with either one. You can connect the other later and read both from Mawkingbird.

After signing in, see [Connections](../connections/index.md) to add the other network, and [Add, switch, or remove an account](../connections/accounts.md) if you want to keep several separate accounts in this browser.

## If you do not have an account

You can [look around without an account](look-around.md). Mawkingbird does not require you to register before you can see how it works.

If you want a new Mastodon account, the Mastodon sign-in page can send you to a server's own registration page. Registration happens there. Return to Mawkingbird after the account is ready.

## Your password stays with your account provider

The normal sign-in buttons take you to Mastodon or Bluesky to approve Mawkingbird. Enter your account password only on the page belonging to that service. Mawkingbird does not see that password.
