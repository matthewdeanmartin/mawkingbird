# Mastodon

Choose **Fedi** in the Apps menu to read your Mastodon Home feed, reply to people you follow and publish from your own account.

## Get to your Home feed

If you already have a Mastodon account, [sign in with your server](../start/sign-in-mastodon.md). If you're new, [choose a server](../new-to-mastodon/choose-a-server.md) and [create an account](../new-to-mastodon/create-an-account.md). You can talk to people on other servers from the same account.

## Give yourself something to read

[Find people by interest](../finding-people/mastodon-hashtags.md), open a profile and choose **Follow**. Return to **Home** to see new posts from the people you follow.

## Join in

Choose **Reply** on a post to join the conversation. To start your own, [write a post](../posting/basic-post.md) and [choose who can see it](../posting/visibility.md).
