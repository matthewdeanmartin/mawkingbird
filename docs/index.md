# Mawkingbird Help

Which part of Mawkingbird are you using? Choose a guide below. In the app, **Apps** at the end of the top menu lets you switch between activities.

- [Mastodon](mastodon/index.md) — choose a server, find people and join a conversation.
- [Bluesky](bluesky/index.md) — sign in with your handle, read your timeline and post to Bluesky.
- [Blog](blog/index.md) — read long Mastodon posts as articles and write your own.
- [Radio](radio/index.md) — find recordings, save singles and play a playlist.
- [Twitter to Mastodon (Unbird)](unbird/index.md) — open your Twitter archive and find people you used to follow.
- [RSS](rss/index.md) — follow a website or blog in your reader.
- [Video](video/index.md) — watch video posts and follow YouTube channels.
- [Forum](reading/forum.md) — follow a conversation from its first post through the replies.
- [Tools](tools/index.md) — pick a handle, find friends across networks or schedule posts.

For settings and features used across apps, browse [shared guides](start/welcome.md), [connections](connections/index.md) or the [glossary](glossary.md).
