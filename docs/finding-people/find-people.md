# Find people to follow

Following someone means their future posts can appear in your Home feed. You can unfollow them later without deleting or blocking their account.

Open **More → Find Friends** to see all of Mawkingbird's discovery choices in one place.

The available Starter Kits, collections, and RSS kits are growing catalogs. Their subjects and membership will change over time; the methods in these guides stay the same.

## Start with a starter kit

A starter kit is a suggested group of accounts. It is the easiest choice when you want an interesting feed but do not know any account names yet.

1. On **Find Friends**, choose **Starter kits**.
2. Pick a subject that interests you.
3. Review the accounts in the kit.
4. Follow the accounts you want.

You are choosing suggestions, not joining a permanent group. You can unfollow any account later.

## Search by subject

Choose one of the suggested interests on **Find Friends**, or choose **Search posts for anything else**. Search for a subject you would enjoy reading about.

Open a promising post, visit its author's profile, and choose **Follow** if you want future posts from that person.

This works well when you know what interests you but not who talks about it.

## Search for a person

Use **Search for people by name** when you already know a name or handle. A handle often looks like `@name@server.example` or `name.bsky.social`.

Check the profile before following. Display names are not unique, so the handle, biography, and recent posts help confirm that you found the intended person.

## Other ways to find people

The advanced choices on **Find Friends** can:

- browse a server's public profile directory;
- open directories maintained elsewhere;
- look for names from your contacts;
- import a follow list from another account;
- help you invite people you already know.

You do not need these choices to get started. Starter kits and subject search are usually faster.

## Anonymous follows

You can follow people while browsing anonymously. Those follows belong to the current browser rather than to an online account. They may not be available on another device or after browser data is cleared.
