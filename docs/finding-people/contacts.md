# Look for people from your contacts

Mawkingbird can search for possible Mastodon accounts using names from contacts you choose. The results are guesses, so you decide which matches are correct and whom to follow.

## Choose contacts

1. Open **More → Find Friends**.
2. Choose **Look for your contacts**.
3. If **Pick from my contacts** is available, choose it and select only the people you want to search for.
4. Otherwise, choose **Choose contacts CSV** and select a contacts export from your device.
5. Choose **Start searching**.

Distinctive names work better than common ones. Contacts without a usable name may be skipped.

## Review every match

Mawkingbird shows possible accounts and the clues that caused each match.

Before choosing **Follow**:

- compare the name and full handle;
- read the biography;
- inspect profile links and recent posts;
- consider whether the organization or other clues agree;
- leave uncertain matches alone.

There is intentionally no Follow All button for contact matches. A name match is not proof that two people are the same person.

## What happens to contact information

Contacts stay in the current browser. Mawkingbird sends derived search terms to the selected server to look for possible accounts; it does not automatically follow anyone.

When contacts are chosen directly from a device, closing the page forgets the chosen contact list. When a file is loaded, the page uses it in the browser for this search.

If you are browsing anonymously, any follows you choose are also stored only in this browser.
