# Find sites to read with RSS

RSS lets a website publish a stream of new articles or updates. Subscribing in Mawkingbird adds that stream to its RSS reader. It does not follow a social account or notify the publisher.

The RSS Starter Kit catalog will continue to expand. Kits and their feed membership may change, so choose from the descriptions and publishers currently shown in Mawkingbird.

## Add an RSS Starter Kit

1. Choose **RSS** in the main navigation.
2. Choose **Starter kits**.
3. Review a kit's description and listed publications.
4. Choose **Add**.

Mawkingbird subscribes to the kit's feeds and files them in a folder named for the kit. You can install more than one kit and remove individual feeds later through **Manage RSS feeds**.

If a kit is larger than your remaining subscription slots, Mawkingbird offers to raise the limit enough to fit it.

One failing feed does not prevent every other feed in the kit from being added. Read the installation report to see what succeeded.

## Find feeds linked by people you follow

The Starter Kits panel also offers **Feeds from people you follow**.

1. Choose **Look for feeds**.
2. Mawkingbird checks websites linked from the posts currently available in your Home timeline.
3. Review each feed it finds.
4. Choose **Add** beside the feeds you want.

Nothing is subscribed until you choose Add. Some websites do not publish a feed or do not allow Mawkingbird to read it, so an empty result does not mean the search is broken.

## Add one site directly

Choose **Paste a link** on the RSS page when you already know the website or feed address. Mawkingbird checks the address before adding the subscription.
