# Start with a Mawkingbird Starter Kit

A Mawkingbird Starter Kit is a ready-made set of social accounts. It gives a new or empty Home feed something useful to show without requiring you to know account names first.

The catalog will grow over time. Subjects, kit sizes, and members may change, so choose from the descriptions Mawkingbird currently offers.

## Open the catalog

1. Open **More → Find Friends**.
2. Choose **Starter kits**.
3. Choose a kit whose description interests you.

The kit page lists its accounts. Choose a name or handle to inspect that person's profile before following.

## Follow the kit

Choose **Follow all** to add every account in the kit. Mawkingbird shows its progress and reports accounts it could not find or follow.

You do not have to follow the whole set. To choose people individually, open their profiles and use **Follow** there.

Following a kit does not permanently join you to a group. It follows the accounts one by one. You can unfollow any of them later.

## Before choosing Follow all

Review the kit's subject and members. When you are signed in, these are real follow actions, and the accounts may receive follow notifications or requests.

When browsing anonymously, follows stay in the current browser. Nothing is sent from a social account, and the follows may disappear if the browser's saved site data is cleared.

## If an account cannot be followed

Accounts can move, change names, require approval, or disappear after a kit is assembled. A failed member does not prevent the other accounts from being followed.

You can search for the person by their current full handle if you think the kit's copy is out of date.

## Mastodon communities by interest

Mawkingbird also publishes guides to [Mastodon communities by interest](https://mawkingbird.com/communities/): one page per subject, with the hashtags worth following, people grouped by kind, and the servers where that community gathers. You can read them without signing in, and some are available in several languages.

- [Science fiction on Mastodon](https://mawkingbird.com/science-fiction-on-mastodon/)
- [Space and astronomy on Mastodon](https://mawkingbird.com/space-and-astronomy-on-mastodon/)
- [Math on Mastodon](https://mawkingbird.com/math-on-mastodon/)
- [Gardening and houseplants on Mastodon](https://mawkingbird.com/gardening-and-houseplants-on-mastodon/)
- [Plant-based living](https://mawkingbird.com/plant-based-living-on-mastodon/)
- [ESP32 and single-board computers on Mastodon](https://mawkingbird.com/esp32-and-single-board-computers-on-mastodon/)

Each page has a **Preview** of what the hashtags look like right now. Signed in to Mastodon, you can follow the tags and people from the page. Hashtags and people you already follow are marked as followed and left out when you follow several at once. While Mawkingbird follows them, a window shows its progress. Keep the page open until it finishes, or choose **Stop**.

## Bluesky starter packs

Bluesky has starter packs of its own: sets of accounts people make on Bluesky. They are separate from Mawkingbird's Starter Kits. Open **Bluesky starter packs** from the ways to find people.

- **Open a pack:** paste its bsky.app link. You see who made it, what it is for, and everyone in it. Follow people one at a time, or choose **Follow all**.
- **After you follow someone,** Mawkingbird may suggest similar accounts, as bsky.app does.
- **Make a pack:** start from one of your Bluesky lists. The pack gets its own copy of the people on it, up to 150.
- **On a Bluesky profile,** the packs that account made are listed under its header.

Following needs a linked Bluesky account; reading a pack does not.
