# Understand profiles and handles

A profile helps you decide whether an account is the person or publication you intended to find—and whether you want its posts in your Home feed.

## Display name and handle

The **display name** is the prominent name chosen by the account owner. It can contain almost anything and does not have to be unique.

The **handle** is the account's more distinctive address:

- Mastodon: `@name@server.example`
- Bluesky: `name.bsky.social` or a person's own domain

Use the handle when searching, mentioning someone, or checking that two similar profiles are different accounts.

## What a profile can contain

A profile may show:

- a picture, display name, handle, and biography;
- links supplied by the account owner;
- recent posts and media;
- follower and following information;
- accounts the owner features or recommends;
- collections curated by the owner;
- relationship labels such as **Follows you**, **Following**, or **Mutuals**.

Not every account or network supplies every section.

## Featured accounts and collections

A profile's featured accounts are people that profile chose to recommend. They can be a useful path from one trusted account to others in the same community.

Collections group accounts around a curator's purpose. Open a collection to review its members and, when offered, sample their recent posts.

Recommendations are opinions, not identity guarantees. Review each profile before following or sharing sensitive information.

## Open the original profile

The account actions may offer **Open on** followed by the account's home site. This is useful when Mawkingbird cannot display part of a profile or when you want to confirm how it appears at its source.
