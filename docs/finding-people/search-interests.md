# Search for something that interests you

You do not need a person's name to find people worth following. Search for a subject, read posts about it, and follow the authors you want to hear from again.

## Start from Find Friends

1. Open **More → Find Friends**.
2. Choose one of the suggested interests, or choose **Search posts for anything else**.
3. Enter a subject using words people are likely to put in a post.
4. Run the search.
5. Open a useful result and visit its author's profile.
6. Choose **Follow** if you want future posts from that account.

The suggested interests are examples, not the boundaries of what you can search for.

## Search directly

On the Search page:

1. Choose **Mastodon** or **Bluesky** as the network.
2. Choose **Posts** as what to search for.
3. Enter the subject.
4. Choose **Search**.

Post search availability depends on the connected account and network. If **Posts** is unavailable, sign in when offered or try the other connected network.

## Try a hashtag

A hashtag is a subject label beginning with `#`, such as `#Gardening`. Choose **Hashtags** on the Search page when you want the tag itself rather than every post containing an ordinary word.

Opening a tag shows its recent posts. Some Fedi accounts can also follow a tag so matching posts appear in Home.

## Improve an empty search

- Use the words participants use, not a broad category.
- Try a synonym or a related hashtag.
- Search one important phrase rather than a full question.
- Switch networks; the people and searchable posts are not identical.
- Use a Starter Kit or collection when you want curated suggestions instead.
