# Size up new followers

**New people to meet** lists recent followers you don't follow back, with what can be
said about each one.

## Three separate answers

Each person gets three separate answers rather than one number:

- **What other people did.** This counts most: people you follow who follow them, and
  people who reply to them.
- **How they behave.** This counts less, because software can imitate it. Behaviour
  alone never earns **Vouched for**.
- **Warning signs.** These are reasons to take a closer look.

Bios, profile pictures and follower counts count for nothing.

## Reading the labels

- An account marked **automated** is taken at its word. An account without that label
  could still be a bot.
- A quiet account isn't a warning sign. Plenty of people mostly read.
- Your server may hold only a few posts from people on other servers. Their follow
  counts and join dates are whatever their own server reports.
