# Tools

Open the [tools directory](https://mawkingbird.com/tools/) when you have a particular job in mind. Each tool explains which account or files you need.

- **Find people after a move.** [Find your Bluesky follows on Mastodon](https://mawkingbird.com/bluesky-to-mastodon/) or [find your Mastodon follows on Bluesky](https://mawkingbird.com/mastodon-to-bluesky/). If you’re starting from a Twitter archive, use the [Unbird guide](../unbird/index.md).
- **Publish on two networks.** Open [Crosspost](https://mawkingbird.com/crosspost/) and check the selected accounts before publishing.
- **Publish later.** Open [Schedule Mastodon posts](https://mawkingbird.com/schedule-mastodon-posts/) and follow the [scheduling guide](../posting/schedule.md) to review the time and destination.
