# Connect Bluesky

A Bluesky connection merges posts from your Bluesky timeline into Home. You can reply, like, and repost Bluesky posts without leaving Mawkingbird.

## Make an app password

Do not use your regular Bluesky password.

1. On Bluesky, open **Settings → Privacy and security → App Passwords**.
2. Create an app password.
3. If you want Bluesky direct messages in Mawkingbird Chat, allow direct-message access while creating it.
4. Copy the app password.

## Link the account

1. Open **Settings → Connections → Bluesky**.
2. Enter your Bluesky handle, such as `you.bsky.social`.
3. Paste the app password.
4. Choose **Link Bluesky**.

This also works while browsing anonymously. In that mode you can read and respond to Bluesky posts, but Chat is not available.

## Unlink

Return to the connection page and choose **Unlink**. Mawkingbird forgets the saved Bluesky session in this browser.

For complete revocation, also delete the app password in Bluesky's settings. You can make a new app password and reconnect later.
