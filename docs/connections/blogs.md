# Publish to a blog

Mawkingbird can send longer writing from the composer to a connected blog. A blog destination appears only after its connection is ready.

Choose the guide for the blog you already use:

- [Blogger](blogger.md) — sign in with the Google account that owns the blog.
- [Mataroa](mataroa.md) — connect with the key from your Mataroa dashboard.
- [Hugo](hugo.md) — connect an existing Hugo blog kept in a GitHub repository.

You can connect more than one kind of blog. Each remains a separate destination.

## Publish

1. Open the composer.
2. Choose the blog under the destination control.
3. Add the title and writing requested for that destination.
4. Choose whether to publish now or save as a draft when that blog offers the choice.
5. Review the destination, then publish.

Social-post features such as polls, visibility, and content warnings may not apply to a blog. The composer hides or explains options that the selected destination cannot use.

## Read your blog in Mawkingbird

Your Mawkingbird profile shows only your own social posts, never your blog's. To read your blog in Mawkingbird, follow its public feed as you would any other: add it on the **Feeds** page, or use **Add my blog to my home timeline** on the Hugo connection page.
