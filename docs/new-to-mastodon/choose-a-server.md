# Choose a Mastodon server

Picking a server is the one decision Mastodon asks of you that X and Bluesky do not. You can follow people on every server from any of them, and you can move your account later, so the first choice is not permanent.

## If you just want to start

Choose a large, general-purpose server such as `mastodon.social`. It is run by the Mastodon project, open to anyone, and you can follow people everywhere from it.

You can stop reading here and [create an account](create-an-account.md).

## If you want a server that fits you

A smaller server can feel more like a community. Your server's local timeline shows posts from its members, and its moderators share your expectations. Look for:

- **Language and region.** A server in your language makes its local timeline and rules easier to read.
- **A topic you care about.** There are servers for scientists, artists, writers, journalists, programmers, gamers and many other groups.
- **Rules you agree with.** Every server publishes its rules. Read them before joining.
- **Active moderation.** A server's about page usually names its administrators and says how to report problems.
- **Sign-up policy.** Some servers are open; others review each request, which can take a day or more.

## Where to find servers

In Mawkingbird, choose **Log in**, then **Mastodon**, then **I need an account**. Pick a server from the list, or follow the link to the full directory on joinmastodon.org.

You can also browse joinmastodon.org directly. It lists servers that have agreed to its moderation standards.

## You can move later

Mastodon lets you move to a new account on another server and take your followers with you. Your old posts stay on the old server.
