# What Mastodon is

Mastodon is a social network made of thousands of independently run servers that talk to each other. You join one server, and you can still follow and talk to people on all the others.

If you have used Twitter, most of it will feel familiar: short posts, replies, likes, reposts, hashtags and a timeline. The differences are in who runs it and how your feed is built.

## Servers instead of one company

Each Mastodon server is run by a person, a group or an organization. Some are general-purpose, such as `mastodon.social`. Others gather a community around a language, a region, a profession or a hobby.

Your server stores your account, applies its own rules and moderates its members. Servers also decide which other servers they connect to. Most connect to almost everyone.

The wider network of servers is often called the **fediverse**. Mastodon is the best-known software on it. Accounts on compatible services, such as Pixelfed for photos, can follow and be followed from Mastodon.

## Your handle includes your server

A Mastodon handle has two parts: your name and your server. For example, `@ada@example.social` is the account `ada` on the server `example.social`.

When you search for someone, use their full handle. Two people can have the same name on different servers.

## Your Home feed is the people you follow

Mastodon's Home feed shows posts from accounts and hashtags you follow, newest first. It adds no suggested accounts and no ads, so a new account starts with an empty Home feed. You fill it by following people, and a [starter kit](../finding-people/starter-kits.md) is the quickest way to begin.

Servers also have an **Explore** or trending page, where you can see what is popular on that server.

## What Mawkingbird is

Mawkingbird is a client: an app for reading and posting. It is not a Mastodon server and does not hold your account. You sign in to Mawkingbird with an account on a server you chose, and you can switch clients at any time without losing anything.

You can also look around in Mawkingbird before you have an account. See [Look around without an account](../start/look-around.md).
