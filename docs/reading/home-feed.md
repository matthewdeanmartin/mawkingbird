# Understand your Home feed

Your Home feed is a stream of posts gathered from the people and sources you chose. Following more people usually makes it more useful.

## What appears in Home

For a signed-in account, Home normally includes posts from people that account follows. Mawkingbird can also combine posts from connected networks and other sources.

For an Anonymous visitor, follows are kept in the current browser. The feed may start with a small preview, then change as you choose people yourself.

## Read a post

Choose the post or its reply count to open the conversation around it. Choose the author's name or picture to visit their profile.

Common actions include:

- **Reply:** write a response connected to the post.
- **Like:** show appreciation. Mastodon may call this a favourite.
- **Repost:** show the post to your followers. Mastodon may call this a boost; Mawkingbird may also use *retweet* in some controls.
- **Quote:** create a new post that refers to the original.
- **Bookmark:** save the post for yourself.
- **Share:** send or copy the post for use elsewhere.

Not every network supports every action. Mawkingbird shows the actions it can perform for that post.

## Change what you see

Controls near the top of Home can include:

- a choice of feed sources;
- buttons to include or hide reposts and replies;
- a time range such as **Today**, **This week**, or **Everything**;
- **Calm**, which hides posts that read as inflammatory;
- a language picker;
- different views of the loaded feed.

These controls change what Mawkingbird displays. They do not unfollow anyone.

If the feed says posts are hidden, use the offered button or the controls at the top to show them again.

## When Home is empty

If you are not following anyone, choose **Find people to follow**. [Starter kits](../finding-people/find-people.md#start-with-a-starter-kit) are the fastest way to create a useful feed without already knowing account names.

If you do follow people but expected more posts:

1. Check whether replies, reposts, Calm, languages, or the time range are hiding them.
2. Choose **Load more** or **Load older posts** at the end of the feed when offered.
3. Use **Feed Doctor** when Mawkingbird offers it at the end of the feed.
