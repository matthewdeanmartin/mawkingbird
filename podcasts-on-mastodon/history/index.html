Questions

Do I need a Mastodon account?
Not to look, or to play what plays in the browser. Following a show needs an account on any Mastodon server.
Where do the episodes play?
Some shows post audio or video straight to Mastodon. Others link to YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts and similar. Each entry says where its show shares them.
Who is listed?
Accounts that describe themselves as a podcast or podcaster, recently shared an episode, and passed a review by hand. Their posts are not republished here.
How do I get listed or removed?
The lists come from public profiles and are refreshed from time to time. If you would rather not be listed, ask and you will be left out of every later refresh.
