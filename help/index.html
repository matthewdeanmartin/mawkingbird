Mawkingbird Help#

Mawkingbird lets you read, follow, and post across social networks from one place.

You do not need to understand how social networks are built to use it. You can start by looking around, sign in with an account you already have, or learn one task at a time.

New to Mawkingbird?#

Start with Welcome to Mawkingbird. It explains what you are looking at without assuming you have used social media before.

If you want to get moving immediately, follow Your first ten minutes.

New to Mastodon?#

Mastodon is not one website but many connected servers. These guides explain it from the start:

  • What Mastodon is
  • Choose a Mastodon server and create an account
  • Coming to Mastodon from X
  • Use Bluesky and Mastodon together
