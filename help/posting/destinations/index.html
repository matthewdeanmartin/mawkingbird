Posting

Choose where a post is published#

The destination selector appears beneath the writing box when more than one publishing destination is available.

Social destinations#

Fedi publishes through your connected Mastodon-style account.

Bluesky publishes through your connected Bluesky account.

Both publishes the same writing to Fedi and Bluesky. It appears when both accounts are connected.

Choose the destination before adding special features. Changing it later may reveal that an attachment or option cannot travel to the new destination.

What Both does#

Mawkingbird uses the lower text limit so the message can fit both networks. Threads become reply chains on both.

Supported images go to both destinations, but video and audio may go only to Fedi. Polls and content warnings go only to the Fedi copy.

The visibility choice applies to the Fedi copy. The Bluesky copy is public. Never choose Both for a post intended to be followers-only or direct.

Scheduling is not available for Both.

A cross-post can partly succeed#

When you choose Both, Mawkingbird starts both publishing attempts. One copy may succeed even if the other fails.

If Mawkingbird reports a cross-post error, check both profiles before trying again. Otherwise, retrying the whole post can create a duplicate on the destination that already succeeded.

Other destinations#

Blog and paste choices appear only after their related service has been connected. They have different controls and do not accept every social-post feature. Mawkingbird explains incompatible additions beside the composer.
