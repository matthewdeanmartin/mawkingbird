Glossary#

Use this page when Mawkingbird or another social network uses an unfamiliar word.

Account#

The identity you use on a social network. An account has a profile and usually a handle. Mawkingbird can remember more than one account.

Anonymous#

Mawkingbird's browser-only way to look around without signing in. Anonymous follows and preferences are kept in the current browser rather than in an online account.

Bluesky#

A social network that Mawkingbird can connect to. Bluesky handles often look like name.bsky.social or use a person's own domain.

Bookmark#

A post you saved for yourself so you can find it later. Bookmarking is different from liking: it is meant for your own reading list.

Boost, repost, or retweet#

Different names for showing someone else's post to your followers. Mastodon commonly says boost. Bluesky says repost. Some Mawkingbird feed controls use retweet as a familiar general label.

Chat#

A conversation area for messages supported by your connected accounts. Do not assume every social-network message is encrypted or visible only to you and its recipient.

Content warning (CW)#

A short summary or warning placed in front of a post. Readers see the warning first and choose whether to reveal the rest. It can also serve as a subject line for a long post.

Feed or timeline#

A stream of posts. Home is your main feed. Mawkingbird also offers feeds based on lists, tags, searches, servers, and other sources.

Fedi#

A short label Mawkingbird uses for Mastodon and compatible social networks. If the posting destination says Fedi, the post goes through the connected Mastodon-style account rather than Bluesky.

Follow#

Choose to receive someone's future posts in your Home feed. Following is reversible; you can unfollow the account later.

Handle#

An account's distinctive address. A Mastodon handle may look like @name@server.example. A Bluesky handle may look like name.bsky.social. Unlike a display name, a full handle identifies a particular account.

Hashtag or tag#

A word beginning with #, such as #BirdWatching. Tags label a subject and can lead to other posts using the same label.

Like or favourite#

A lightweight way to show appreciation for a post. Mastodon commonly calls this a favourite. Bluesky calls it a like.

Mastodon#

A social network made up of many independently operated servers. People on different Mastodon servers can usually follow and talk with one another.

Mention#

Including someone's handle in a post so the post refers to them and normally notifies them. A mention begins with @.

Post#

A message published to a social network. A post can contain text and may also contain media, a poll, or other additions.

Profile#

The page describing an account. It normally includes a name, handle, picture, short biography, and recent posts.

Quote#

A new post that refers to another post and adds your own words.

Reply#

A post written in response to another post. Replies are connected into a conversation.

Server#

The website that holds a Mastodon account. For the handle @name@example.social, the server is usually example.social. You do not have to understand server administration to use the account.

Starter kit#

A suggested collection of accounts grouped around a subject. It helps fill a new or empty feed without requiring you to know account names in advance.

Thread#

Several connected posts from the same author, usually used when one post is not enough.

Visibility#

A posting choice that affects who can discover or read a post. The choices differ between networks. Visibility is selected for each post and should not be confused with encrypted private messaging.
