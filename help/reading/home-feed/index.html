Reading

Understand your Home feed#

Your Home feed is a stream of posts gathered from the people and sources you chose. Following more people usually makes it more useful.

What appears in Home#

For a signed-in account, Home normally includes posts from people that account follows. Mawkingbird can also combine posts from connected networks and other sources.

A Bluesky-primary account reads its Bluesky timeline without requiring a Mastodon login. Connecting a signed-in Mastodon account adds its Home timeline. A Mastodon-primary account can likewise add a Bluesky connection.

For an Anonymous visitor, follows are kept in the current browser. The feed may start with a small preview, then change as you choose people yourself.

Read a post#

Choose the post or its reply count to open the conversation around it. Choose the author's name or picture to visit their profile.

Common actions include:

  • Reply: write a response connected to the post.
  • Like: show appreciation. Mastodon may call this a favourite.
  • Repost: show the post to your followers. Mastodon may call this a boost; Mawkingbird may also use retweet in some controls.
  • Quote: create a new post that refers to the original.
  • Bookmark: save the post for yourself.
  • Share: send or copy the post for use elsewhere.

Not every network supports every action. Mawkingbird shows the actions it can perform for that post.

Change what you see#

Controls near the top of Home can include:

  • a choice of feed sources;
  • buttons to include or hide reposts and replies;
  • a time range such as Today, This week, or Everything;
  • Calm, which hides posts that read as inflammatory;
  • a language picker;
  • different views of the loaded feed.

These controls change what Mawkingbird displays. They do not unfollow anyone.

If the feed says posts are hidden, use the offered button or the controls at the top to show them again.

When Home is empty#

If you are not following anyone, choose Find people to follow. Starter kits are the fastest way to create a useful feed without already knowing account names.

If you do follow people but expected more posts:

  1. Check whether replies, reposts, Calm, languages, or the time range are hiding them.
  2. Choose Load more or Load older posts at the end of the feed when offered.
  3. Use Feed Doctor when Mawkingbird offers it at the end of the feed.

If Bluesky fails to load, Home shows the failure details and a retry button. A failed request does not mean that nobody has posted.

If Bluesky is your primary account and its saved session is missing or no longer valid, Home offers Sign in to Bluesky again or Cancel. Confirming starts OAuth for the same account without logging out. You can cancel and reopen the dialog from the feed warning. App-password accounts use the Bluesky sign-in form without the logout detour. For Bluesky linked under another primary account, the warning instead points to Settings → Connections → Bluesky.

The diagnostic message “The session was deleted by another process” comes from Bluesky's OAuth library when its saved session is unavailable. The account's profile can still be listed even though its OAuth credentials are gone. Signing in again restores those credentials; the message alone does not identify what removed the session.

Notifications from connected networks#

Notifications offers only the networks you have signed in to. With both Mastodon and Bluesky configured, it starts on Both, combining loaded notifications newest-first. Choose either network to narrow the list; Load more pages each selected network independently.

The friends/followers filters and Accounts New to Me are available in the Mastodon-only view. Mastodon can receive live notifications when auto-refresh is enabled; use Refresh to check Bluesky. If one network fails, the other network's notifications remain visible alongside an error message.
