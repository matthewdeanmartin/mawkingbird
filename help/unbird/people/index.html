Twitter to Mastodon (Unbird)

Find your Twitter follows on Mastodon#

After you open your archive, Unbird searches for possible Mastodon accounts. Open People to review the results.

Compare each suggested profile with the person you remember. A familiar display name alone can belong to someone else. Open the profile and check its links or bio before you choose Follow with your Mastodon account.

If you’re still choosing a server, the public importer shows where the people it found have landed. You can choose a server and still follow people on other servers.

To look through your old posts instead, choose Archive in Unbird.
