Radio

Radio#

Open Apps → Radio to listen to recordings shared in posts. A single is one recording. A playlist collects recordings you can play in sequence.

Find your first single#

  1. Open Singles and choose the Find tab.
  2. Choose Tags to look for music under a hashtag such as #fedimusic, or Links if you already have a link to a recording. Sign in to search your Home feed or lists.
  3. Run the search and choose Save single beside a recording you want to keep.
  4. Return to the Saved tab and use the play control next to the title. Some sources open their own player.

To listen to several recordings in order, make a playlist.
