Sign in with Mastodon#

To sign in, you need to know the Mastodon server where your account lives. It is usually the website you already use for Mastodon.

For example, the account @ada@example.social normally lives on example.social.

Sign in#

  1. Open Mawkingbird and choose Log in.
  2. Choose Mastodon.
  3. In Your server, enter the server's name, such as mastodon.social. Do not enter your full profile address.
  4. Wait for Mawkingbird to say it connected to that server.
  5. Choose Full access if you want to post, reply, and follow from Mawkingbird. Choose Read only if you only want to browse.
  6. Choose Sign in with followed by your server's name.
  7. Sign in on your server's own page and approve access.

Your server sends you back to Mawkingbird. Your Home feed can then show posts from people that account follows.

If you do not know your server#

Look at the website or app where you normally use Mastodon. The website name after the @ in your full handle is often the server name.

If you have not joined Mastodon yet, use the server directory linked from the sign-in page. Pick a server, create the account on that server, and then return to Mawkingbird to sign in.

If the server cannot be reached#

Check the spelling first. Enter only a website name such as example.social, without an @, username, or profile path.

The server may also be temporarily unavailable or blocked on your network. Use Try again after a moment. If Mawkingbird warns that images cannot load, you can decide whether to continue without images or try again later.
