Start here

Look around without an account#

You can read public posts and try many parts of Mawkingbird before signing in.

Start exploring#

  1. Open mawkingbird.com.
  2. Choose Continue without logging in.
  3. Mawkingbird opens the Home feed using a temporary local identity called Anonymous.

If Mawkingbird cannot reach the public server it tried, it will offer to find another working server. Choose one of the working choices to continue. You do not need an account on that server just to read public posts.

What you can do#

While browsing anonymously, you can:

  • read public posts;
  • search for people, posts, and topics;
  • follow people for a feed that is personal to this browser;
  • use starter kits to fill an empty feed;
  • bookmark posts locally;
  • change appearance and reading preferences;
  • practice chatting with the built-in helper when it is available.

Some actions require a signed-in account. Mawkingbird will tell you when you reach one. Publishing to Mastodon, receiving account notifications, and interacting as yourself are examples.

What “kept in this browser” means#

Anonymous follows and preferences stay on this device in this browser. They are not attached to an online account. Clearing this browser's saved site data, using a private window, or moving to another device may make them disappear.

Sign in later#

Choose Login in the main navigation when you are ready. Signing in gives Mawkingbird access to the feed and actions allowed by your existing account.
