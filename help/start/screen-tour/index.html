A quick tour of the screen#

Mawkingbird keeps the places you will use most in the main navigation. On a narrow screen, some labels may be represented by their icons.

Main navigation#

Home shows posts gathered for you, usually from people you follow.

Algo offers a ranked view intended to surface interesting posts rather than simply showing them by time.

Inbox shows account activity such as replies and other notifications. It appears when your signed-in account supports it.

Chat holds conversations. What appears here depends on the accounts and connections you use.

Search finds people, posts, and subjects.

Feeds collects other ways to read, including lists, saved searches, server feeds, collections, and tags.

RSS is for reading sites that publish an RSS feed. You can ignore it if you do not use RSS.

Login appears while you are looking around without a signed-in account.

The More menu#

More holds destinations you may use less often. These include Settings, Likes, Bookmarks, Drafts, Find Friends, and Docs. Other entries appear when their related feature is available.

Your account menu#

Open the account menu by choosing your name or picture. From here you can visit your profile, switch between saved accounts, add another account, or log out.

See Add, switch, or remove an account for the details.

The Home feed#

The center of Home contains the writing buttons, feed controls, and posts. Other areas may suggest people to follow or subjects that are active.

Mawkingbird adapts this layout to the width of your screen. If a side area is absent, its important destinations remain available through the main navigation or More menu.
