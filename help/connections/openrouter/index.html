Connections and accounts

Connect OpenRouter for writing help#

OpenRouter gives Mawkingbird optional assistance with tasks such as turning a plain-language idea into a Mastodon search and suggesting active hashtags. Its services are billed by usage.

You do not need this connection to read, search, or post normally.

Connect#

  1. Open Settings → Connections → OpenRouter.
  2. Choose Connect OpenRouter.
  3. Sign in or create an OpenRouter account when prompted.
  4. Approve the connection and return to Mawkingbird.

Review your available Credits before using assisted features.

Choose a model#

Under Model, search or browse the available choices. Compare the displayed price and capabilities, then choose one. Prices and availability can change, so check the current details before using a model heavily.

Translation and prompts#

The Translation choice controls whether Mawkingbird may translate text with the selected model. Leave it off if you do not want text sent for translation.

The Prompts area lets you adjust the instructions used for individual assistance features. Use Reset to return to Mawkingbird's original wording if an edit produces poor results.

Disconnect#

Choose Disconnect on the connection page. Check OpenRouter's own account page for remaining credits, usage, and keys.
