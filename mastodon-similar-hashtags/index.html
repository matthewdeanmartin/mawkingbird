Questions

Which hashtags should I use on Mastodon?

Mastodon has no algorithm deciding who sees your post: people find it by following a hashtag or searching for it. Use the hashtags those people already use, which is what this list shows, and write them in CamelCase (#DogsOfMastodon) so screen readers can say them.

How is "similar" worked out?

By counting. Every hashtag on the sampled posts is tallied, once per post, and the ones you asked about are left out. A hashtag on 40 of 200 posts ranks above one on 5.

Why compare several hashtags at once?

People split one topic across spellings: #dog and #dogs, #photo and #photography. Entering them together samples posts carrying any of them, so the list covers the whole conversation.

Why do the results differ between servers?

Each Mastodon server only sees posts from people its own users follow, so a big, busy server gives the broadest picture. Signed in, the sample comes from your own server, which shows what your corner of the network uses.
