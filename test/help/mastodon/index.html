Mastodon

Mastodon#

Choose Fedi in the Apps menu to read your Mastodon Home feed, reply to people you follow and publish from your own account.

Get to your Home feed#

If you already have a Mastodon account, sign in with your server. If you're new, choose a server and create an account. You can talk to people on other servers from the same account.

Give yourself something to read#

Find people by interest, open a profile and choose Follow. Return to Home to see new posts from the people you follow.

Join in#

Choose Reply on a post to join the conversation. To start your own, write a post and choose who can see it.
