Finding people

Start with a Mawkingbird Starter Kit#

A Mawkingbird Starter Kit is a ready-made set of social accounts. It gives a new or empty Home feed something useful to show without requiring you to know account names first.

The catalog will grow over time. Subjects, kit sizes, and members may change, so choose from the descriptions Mawkingbird currently offers.

Open the catalog#

  1. Open More → Find Friends.
  2. Choose Starter kits.
  3. Choose a kit whose description interests you.

The kit page lists its accounts. Choose a name or handle to inspect that person's profile before following.

Follow the kit#

Choose Follow all to add every account in the kit. Mawkingbird shows its progress and reports accounts it could not find or follow.

You do not have to follow the whole set. To choose people individually, open their profiles and use Follow there.

Following a kit does not permanently join you to a group. It follows the accounts one by one. You can unfollow any of them later.

Before choosing Follow all#

Review the kit's subject and members. When you are signed in, these are real follow actions, and the accounts may receive follow notifications or requests.

When browsing anonymously, follows stay in the current browser. Nothing is sent from a social account, and the follows may disappear if the browser's saved site data is cleared.

If an account cannot be followed#

Accounts can move, change names, require approval, or disappear after a kit is assembled. A failed member does not prevent the other accounts from being followed.

You can search for the person by their current full handle if you think the kit's copy is out of date.
