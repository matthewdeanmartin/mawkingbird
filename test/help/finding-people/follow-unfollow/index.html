Finding people

Follow, request, or unfollow#

Following an account asks for its future posts to appear in your Home feed. You remain in control: you can unfollow it later without blocking or reporting it.

Follow an account#

  1. Open the account's profile or find its row in search or a directory.
  2. Choose Follow.
  3. Wait for the button to update.

Following means the follow is active.

Requested means the account requires approval. The account owner can accept or decline the request. Until it is accepted, you are not yet following the account.

Mutuals means you follow each other.

Following is a visible social action. A signed-in follow or request may notify the account owner.

Unfollow an account#

  1. Open the account's profile.
  2. Choose Following or Mutuals.
  3. Confirm the unfollow.

Their future posts stop being included because of that follow. Posts already loaded, bookmarked, quoted, or present through another feed may still appear.

Unfollowing does not block the account. You can still visit its public profile, and it may still follow or mention you.

Follow all from a kit or collection#

Bulk follow buttons create individual follows for every eligible account. Review the subject and members first. Some accounts may require approval or fail because they moved or are unavailable.

Anonymous follows#

When browsing anonymously, following is a local choice stored in the current browser. It changes your Mawkingbird feed but sends no follow from an online social account.

These follows may disappear if you clear saved site data or use another browser or device.
