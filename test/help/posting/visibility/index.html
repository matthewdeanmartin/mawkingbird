Posting

Choose who can see a post#

Visibility controls who can discover or read a post sent to Fedi. Check it before publishing; a label that sounds private may not provide the privacy you expect.

Fedi visibility choices#

The composer offers four choices:

public

Anyone can read the post. It can appear on your profile, in public feeds, and in search or discovery features. Your followers receive it in their Home feeds.

unlisted

The post is still public. Anyone with the link can read it, and it appears on your profile and in followers' feeds. It is kept out of some public discovery areas. This is sometimes called quiet public.

private

The post is intended for your followers and people you mention. It does not appear in public feeds. Mawkingbird may describe this choice as followers only elsewhere.

direct

The post is intended only for accounts mentioned in it. Add every intended recipient with an @handle.

Private and direct are not encrypted#

Do not use a private or direct post for passwords, financial details, medical records, or other secrets. The servers involved in delivering the post may be able to read it.

These choices limit delivery; they do not turn social media into a secure messaging service.

Bluesky posts are public#

Bluesky posts are public. If the destination is Bluesky, the visibility selector does not make the Bluesky post private.

If the destination is Both, the selected visibility applies to the Fedi copy. The Bluesky copy remains public. Do not use Both for something intended only for followers or mentioned people.

Change the usual choice#

You can choose visibility separately for each post. To change the starting choice used by new Fedi posts, open More → Settings → Writing and change Posting privacy.
