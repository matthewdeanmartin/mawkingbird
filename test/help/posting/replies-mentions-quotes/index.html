Posting

Reply, mention, or quote#

These three actions connect your words to another person or post, but they do different jobs.

Reply to a post#

Use a reply when you are continuing the conversation beneath a particular post.

  1. Find the post.
  2. Choose Reply.
  3. Write your response in the box that opens.
  4. Leave the author's leading @handle in place if you want to notify them.
  5. Choose Reply.

Mawkingbird sends the reply through the network where the original post belongs. You do not need to choose a destination.

Removing the leading handle may let you write into the conversation without sending that person a mention notification. Mawkingbird warns you when this applies.

Mention someone#

Use a mention when you want to refer to or notify an account in a new post.

Type the person's full handle in your message. A Mastodon handle may look like @name@server.example; a Bluesky handle may look like name.bsky.social.

Check the handle carefully. Display names are not unique, and mentioning the wrong handle can notify the wrong person.

Starting a new post with a mention is not the same as using the Reply button. It creates a new post rather than attaching your words to an existing conversation.

Quote a post#

Use a quote when you want to create a new post for your audience while referring to another post.

  1. Open the post's sharing actions.
  2. Choose Quote when it is available.
  3. Add your own words.
  4. Check the visibility.
  5. Choose Quote.

Not every post can be quoted. The author, their network, or your connection may restrict the action. A quote is a fresh statement to your audience, so read the original carefully and add enough context for your readers.
