Posting

Fix, edit, or delete a post#

Act carefully when correcting a published post. Editing preserves the post; deleting removes it. Not every connected network offers the same choices.

Catch a mistake before it is sent#

If Mawkingbird shows a Posting in 30s countdown, choose Cancel. Your text remains available to edit. Choose Publish now only when you are sure.

The countdown appears when delayed sending is enabled in your preferences.

Edit a Fedi post#

  1. Find your post.
  2. Choose its pencil-shaped Edit action.
  3. Correct the text.
  4. Save the edit.

Other people may be able to view the post's edit history. Editing is not a way to erase something that was previously published.

Correct a Bluesky post#

  1. Choose the pencil-shaped Edit action on your post.
  2. Correct the text, then choose Delete and repost.
  3. Review the current replies, reposts, likes and quotes in the confirmation. Choose Cancel to keep the original, or confirm to publish the replacement.

The replacement keeps the original attachments, languages, content labels and reply context, but has a new link. Existing engagement does not move to it; replies and quotes still reference the deleted original. The original is only deleted as part of the same transaction that publishes the replacement. If a network error makes the outcome uncertain, your edited text stays available; check your profile before retrying.

Delete a post#

  1. Find your post.
  2. Choose the trash-shaped Delete action.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Deletion asks the connected network to remove the post. Copies, screenshots, notifications, quotes, or reposts that already reached other people may remain elsewhere.

Delete and redraft#

Fedi posts may offer Delete and redraft. This removes the original first, then opens its text for changes and reposting.

Use it only when you are ready to continue immediately. If you cancel after the deletion, the original is already gone. Copy important text elsewhere before using this action.

Check every destination#

If the post was published to Both, editing or deleting one copy does not guarantee that the other copy changed. Visit both profiles and correct each published copy as needed.
