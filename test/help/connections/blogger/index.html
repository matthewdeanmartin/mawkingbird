Connections and accounts

Connect a Blogger blog#

The Blogger connection publishes to a Google Blogger blog from the composer. It can publish immediately or save a draft.

Connect and choose a blog#

  1. Open Settings → Connections → Blog (Blogger).
  2. Choose Sign in with Google.
  3. Sign in with the Google account that owns the blog and approve the requested Blogger access.
  4. Back in Mawkingbird, choose Refresh blog list.
  5. Choose the blog you want under Publish to.

Nothing can publish until a blog is selected. If your Google account owns several blogs, return here to change the destination.

Google may say that the app has not been verified. Read the warning and permission request carefully. If you are not comfortable continuing, cancel; the rest of Mawkingbird still works without Blogger.

Publish or include the blog on your profile#

Choose the selected blog as the destination in the composer.

Under On your profile, turn on Include my blog's posts in my profile feed if you want its public posts shown alongside your Mawkingbird posts. This may require the Mawkingbird proxy.

Sign out or forget the blog#

Choose Sign out to end the Google session. The session lasts only for the current tab, so signing in again later is normal.

Choose Forget this blog to remove the remembered destination and profile-feed choice.
