Connections and accounts

Read public Twitter accounts#

The Twitter connection is read-only. It lets you follow public Twitter accounts inside Mawkingbird. You do not sign in to Twitter, and likes or replies in Mawkingbird are not sent there.

This connection uses a separate paid data service. Check its prices and limits before creating an account or buying credits.

Check your network first#

Open Settings → Connections → Connection doctor and run the check. Confirm that your browser can reach the Twitter data service and the Mawkingbird proxy before paying for anything.

Connect#

  1. Open Settings → Connections → Twitter.
  2. Read Read this first, then choose one of the listed services.
  3. Create a key with that service and paste it into the key field.
  4. Choose Save connection.
  5. Set up the Mawkingbird proxy requested on the page.
  6. Review the notice about sending requests through the proxy and approve it only if you are comfortable.
  7. Choose Test connection.

Mawkingbird displays request use and estimated cost. The limits are guardrails, not guarantees from the provider. Watch the provider's own billing page too.

Follow accounts#

Under Accounts you follow, enter a public handle such as @NASA. Review the account Mawkingbird finds, then confirm the follow.

You can also paste several handles or Twitter profile addresses at once. The import tool can inspect a public account's following list; nothing is followed until you review and keep it.

Stop or disconnect#

Turn off an account in the followed list to pause its refreshes, or choose Unfollow to remove it.

Choose Disconnect beside the data service to forget its key. Also revoke the key at the service if you no longer plan to use it.
