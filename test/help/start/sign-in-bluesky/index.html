Sign in with Bluesky#

Use your existing Bluesky account to bring its timeline and actions into Mawkingbird.

Sign in#

  1. Open Mawkingbird and choose Log in.
  2. Choose Bluesky.
  3. Choose Continue with Bluesky.
  4. On the Bluesky sign-in page, enter the email address or username you use for Bluesky.
  5. Approve access for Mawkingbird.

Bluesky returns you to Mawkingbird. Your Bluesky timeline becomes your Home feed, and you can reply, like, and repost from Mawkingbird.

Your password is entered on Bluesky's page. Mawkingbird does not see it.

Use a particular account#

If the normal button chooses the wrong account, expand the advanced sign-in choice. Enter your Bluesky handle, such as yourname.bsky.social, and continue.

Most people do not need the other sign-in choices on this page.

If Bluesky asks you to sign in again#

Complete the sign-in on Bluesky's page, then approve Mawkingbird. If the page reports an error, return to Mawkingbird and try Continue with Bluesky again.

People whose account provider does not yet support the normal approval flow can use the compatibility option on the page. Follow its instructions and create a separate app password. Never paste your regular Bluesky password into that compatibility form.
