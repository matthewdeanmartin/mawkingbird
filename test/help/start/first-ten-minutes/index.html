Your first ten minutes#

This is the shortest path from opening Mawkingbird to having a useful feed and publishing your first post.

1. Enter Mawkingbird#

Sign in with Mastodon or Bluesky. If you are not ready, look around without an account instead.

2. Find a few people#

Choose Find people to follow from the Home feed, or open More → Find Friends.

Start with Starter kits if you do not know anyone yet. A starter kit offers a ready-made group of accounts around a subject. You can review the group and follow the accounts that interest you.

You can also search for a subject. Open a few profiles and choose Follow when you want their future posts in your Home feed.

See Find people to follow for the other choices.

3. Read your Home feed#

Choose Home in the main navigation. Newer posts from people you follow appear here.

On a post, you can open its replies, visit the author's profile, reply, like, repost, quote, share, or save it. The exact actions available depend on the account and network connected to Mawkingbird.

See Understand your Home feed when you want to change what appears.

4. Publish a simple post#

  1. On Home, choose Quick post.
  2. Write a short message.
  3. Check the destination and visibility shown below the writing box.
  4. Choose Post.

If Quick post is not available, choose Write. Your writing is saved as a draft first; open More → Drafts when you are ready to publish it.

See Write and publish a basic post for a careful explanation.

5. Learn only what you need next#

You now know enough to use Mawkingbird. Explore the rest when a need comes up. The screen tour explains each main destination, and the glossary explains unfamiliar labels.
