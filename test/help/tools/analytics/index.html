How the analytics numbers work#

Mastodon does not publish view counts. Every number on the analytics pages comes from public data: follower counts, posts, favourites, boosts, replies and quotes. This page explains how each one is calculated.

What gets counted#

  • Sample. Account analytics reads an account's latest posts, about 100 of them (more if you fetch more pages), along with the boosts in between. In the desktop app it reads the local archive instead. Feed analytics reads the posts in that feed. The numbers describe this sample, not the account's whole history. Boosts count toward the activity mix, style and liveliness. Their favourites and boosts belong to someone else, so they are left out of the engagement and reach figures.
  • Thread. One post plus the author's own replies chained under it. A reply to someone else is not part of the author's thread and does not count toward their reach.
  • Counts are what the account's server reports when the page loads. Favourites from servers it has never heard from are missing.

Reach#

Est. reach is the number of people who probably saw a thread.

Could have reached is everyone the thread could have reached: the author's followers, plus the followers of each person who boosted it.

The estimate uses the method published by FediWings. That method is fitted to view counts from 510 Instagram stories:

min(G, max(0.0165 × G × (1 + 2B + 2Q)^0.7314 × (1 + L + R)^0.2214, L + B + Q))

G is Could have reached, B is boosts, Q is quotes, L is favourites and R is replies. A boost or a quote counts as two shares. The result is never more than G and never fewer than the people who reacted.

There are two ways to work out G:

Quick reach Full reach
Boosters Each boost counts as one typical booster with 268 followers The real boosters' follower counts, each counted once per thread
API calls None One per boosted post (up to five pages in the desktop app)
Where Everywhere, by default Web: after you click Refine reach. Desktop app: automatic, saved in the archive

The 268 comes from a calibration on five public accounts of different sizes. Each account's median booster audience was taken, then the median of those five. Boosts whose booster the server does not list also count as 268.

People who follow both the author and a booster are counted twice in Could have reached. For accounts with more than about 100,000 followers, the estimate reaches the upper limit, so Est. reach equals Could have reached.

Account analytics#

Stat How it is calculated
Favourites, Boosts, Replies received Sum over the posts in the sample
Engagements per post Favourites + boosts + replies received, divided by posts
Replies given Share of the account's own posts that reply to someone else; continuing their own thread does not count
Shortest / longest post Visible characters, including any content warning
Posts per day Posts divided by the days between the newest and oldest post in the sample
Liveliness 0–100 from how recently and how often they posted or boosted; 40+ is Active, 10–39 Slowing, under 10 Dormant
Recent posts / day Posts in the last 30 days, divided by 30
Effective followers Optional scan of the follower list: followers who posted recently

Style and signals#

Stat How it is calculated
Own vs boosted Their own posts (new posts, thread posts, replies) against boosts, out of everything in the sample
New posts, Thread posts, Replies to others Each as a share of everything in the sample
Link-only posts Share of their own posts that are a link or preview card with under 40 characters of their own text
Style The first that fits: Curator (boosts ≥ 50%), Conversationalist (replies to others ≥ 50%), Link sharer (link-only ≥ 50% of own posts), Thread writer (thread posts ≥ 30% of own posts), Broadcaster (replies < 10% and boosts < 30%), otherwise Balanced. Needs 20 statuses.
Posting rhythm Human, Partly scheduled or Bot-like (see below)
Bot setting The account's own "This is an automated account" setting

Posting rhythm looks for posts that arrive on a clock. A run is 4 or more posts in a row with the same gap between them, to within 2 minutes or 1% (every hour, every 24 hours). Thread posts and boosts are left out, because people produce even gaps when writing a thread or boosting in one sitting, and gaps under 5 minutes do not count. With under 10% of its posts in runs, the account is Human. From 10% to under 60%, it is Partly scheduled; with 60% or more, it is Bot-like. The profile page shows a Bot-like or Partly scheduled label next to the official Bot label, judged from the posts loaded there. It needs at least 8 posts.

People who react (desktop app, your account)#

Counted from your notifications for the last 90 days: favourites, boosts, quotes, replies and mentions.

Stat How it is calculated
People Different accounts that reacted
Servers Different servers those accounts are on
Returning People who reacted in two or more of the last three calendar months
Top 5 share Share of all reactions from the five most active people

On the web, after Refine reach, Different boosters shows how many different people boosted the threads in the sample, and on how many servers.

Post analytics#

Open More tools → Post analytics on any Mastodon post. The page reads the post and its conversation (two requests). Replies is the server's count. Visible here is the number of replies by other people that your server shows.

In the desktop app, the page also shows when the boosts and favourites of your own posts arrived. This comes from your notifications. Notifications collected before this feature existed are not linked to a post.

Desktop app history#

The desktop app keeps your posts in a local archive. Each time you open analytics, it reads new posts and re-reads the last 14 days so the counts stay current. It reads at most 10 pages, one a second. If it stops early, it continues from the same place next time.

  • Last 30 days vs the 30 before adds up the threads started in each window.
  • By month covers the last 12 months. Months the archive only partly covers are marked.
  • Followers records your follower count once a day and counts new follows from your follow notifications. Servers do not report unfollows.

Feed analytics#

Hashtag, list and Home analytics describe the sampled posts. Every section says what it counts. Top 5 authors' share is the share of the sampled posts written by the five most frequent authors. No engagement is the share of posts with no favourites, boosts or replies that your server has seen.
