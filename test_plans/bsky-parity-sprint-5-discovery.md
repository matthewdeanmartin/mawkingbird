# Sprint 5: Bluesky starter packs and discovery

Written for: Bluesky parity sprint 5 (2026-11-03). Status: not run.

## Setup

**Bluesky A** linked (as a Bluesky-first account, then as Mastodon M with the
connector). On bsky.app as A: make one list of people (Lists → New list) with a
few accounts. Have a public starter pack link handy (any `bsky.app/starter-pack/…`).

## Steps

### Finding it

- [ ] 1. Find people (basic) shows "On Bluesky too? Open Bluesky starter
      packs" below the usual options. The Starter Kits page has a similar line.
      Both open `/bluesky/starter-packs`.
- [ ] 2. Home's invitation cards are unchanged (no new card).

### Opening a pack

- [ ] 3. Paste the pack link and choose Open. The pack page shows its name,
      "A Bluesky starter pack by @…", description, people count and joined count,
      and everyone in it.
- [ ] 4. "Open on bsky.app" opens the same pack there.
- [ ] 5. Follow one person: their button turns to Following and a "Similar to
      <name>" list appears with a few accounts not in the pack.
- [ ] 6. **Follow all N**: N counts only people you do not follow yet (never
      yourself). After it, everyone shows Following and a notice says how many
      were followed. bsky.app agrees.
- [ ] 7. Signed in with no Bluesky link (Mastodon only): the pack is readable,
      with no Follow buttons and a "Link Bluesky" prompt.
- [ ] 8. Paste a non-pack link: a clear error.

### Making a pack

- [ ] 9. Make a starter pack: name, description, choose your list, submit. You
      land on the new pack's page with the list's people.
- [ ] 10. On bsky.app, the pack exists under your profile's starter packs, with
      the same people. Editing your original list later does not change the pack.
- [ ] 11. With no lists on your account, the section says to make one on bsky.app.

### Profiles and suggestions

- [ ] 12. Open the profile of someone who made a starter pack: "Starter packs by
      <name>" lists them, linking to Mawkingbird's pack page.
- [ ] 13. Follow a Bluesky account from its profile: "Similar to <name>" appears
      below the header.
- [ ] 14. The hub's "Suggested for you" lists accounts with Follow buttons.

### Show more / less like this

- [ ] 15. Pin a feed that takes feedback (bsky.app shows "Show more like this"
      on its posts; Discover does). Open it in Mawkingbird. A post's ••• has
      **Show more like this** and **Show less like this**.
- [ ] 16. Choose one: it turns into "✓ The feed will show … like this" and both
      are disabled for that post.
- [ ] 17. A feed without feedback (most custom feeds) shows neither item.
