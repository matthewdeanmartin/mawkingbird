# Sprint 1: Bluesky video

Written for: Bluesky parity sprint 1 (2026-11-02). Status: not run.

## Setup

**Bluesky A** with a verified email, linked to Mawkingbird. Have ready: a short
MP4 (under a minute), a WebM, a photo, a file over 300 MB if you can, and a
video over 10 minutes if you can.

## Steps

- [ ] 1. Composer, post to **Bluesky** only. Attach the MP4. No warning appears,
      and the video previews in the composer.
- [ ] 2. Add alt text. Post. The notice shows "Uploading the video to Bluesky…",
      then "Bluesky is processing the video (…%)…".
- [ ] 3. The post appears on bsky.app with the video playing, the right aspect
      ratio, and the alt text (check with a screen reader or bsky.app's ALT
      badge).
- [ ] 4. The same post plays in Mawkingbird's Home.
- [ ] 5. Post to **Both** with a video: the Mastodon copy and the Bluesky copy
      both carry it.
- [ ] 6. Quote someone's Bluesky post with a video attached: the quote shows the
      video and the quoted post.
- [ ] 7. Attach a photo, then try to add a video: refused, "photos or a video,
      not both".
- [ ] 8. Pick two videos at once: refused, "one video per post".
- [ ] 9. Pick a file over 300 MB: refused at once. Pick a video over 10 minutes:
      refused after a moment.
- [ ] 10. With an account whose email is **not** verified (or after the daily
      limit), post a video: the error names Bluesky's reason and nothing is
      posted to Bluesky.
- [ ] 11. Network tab: the video bytes go to `video.bsky.app`, not to any
      Mawkingbird or proxy host.
