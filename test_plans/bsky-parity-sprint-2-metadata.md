# Sprint 2: engagement lists and post metadata

Written for: Bluesky parity sprint 2 (2026-11-02). Status: not run.

## Setup

**Bluesky A** linked; **Bluesky B** available to like and repost.

## Steps

### Who engaged

- [ ] 1. On a busy Bluesky post, tap the like count after ⭐: a list of likers
      opens, each linking to their profile.
- [ ] 2. Tap the repost count after 🔁: the reposters open.
- [ ] 3. Signed out with no Bluesky account (anonymous), open a Bluesky post:
      the lists still open.

### Post languages

- [ ] 4. Compose to Bluesky, set the post language to German, write German text,
      post. On bsky.app (or via the post's record) the language is `de`.

### Link cards

- [ ] 5. Post to Bluesky with only text and a link to a news article. bsky.app
      shows a card with title, description and image.
- [ ] 6. Post a link to a page that does not exist. The post still goes out,
      without a card, within about 8 seconds.
- [ ] 7. Post a link plus a photo: the photo wins, no card.

### Content labels

- [ ] 8. Compose to Bluesky with a photo and press ⚠️. A **Bluesky label** menu
      and a hint appear. Choose "Bluesky: nudity" (use a harmless photo).
- [ ] 9. Post. On bsky.app as Bluesky B the photo is blurred behind a warning.
- [ ] 10. In Mawkingbird, as Bluesky A or anonymous, the same post's media is
      covered until you choose to show it.
- [ ] 11. A post labelled by a moderation service (find one with a known label)
      is also covered in Mawkingbird.
