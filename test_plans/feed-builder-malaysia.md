# Malaysia regional kit

Open `/communities/?c=international-regions&lang=ms`,
`/ms/komuniti-mastodon/` and `/ms/malaysia-di-mastodon/`.
Check the Malaysia link under International Regions, Malay page controls,
25 accounts and no hashtags. Copy must describe English, Malay and mixed posts,
quiet accounts and unofficial news feeds without promising frequent activity.
The Malay hub's tools link must open `/tools/`, not a nonexistent `/ms/` index.

Preview selected accounts on mobile and desktop. An empty recent sample is
possible for quiet accounts. With a test account, follow a selection and convert
it to a list named Malaysia. Signed-in mutations remain manual.

This regional kit's small-community exceptions do not change ordinary English
SIG eligibility. App-wide UI locales and general starter packs are unchanged.
