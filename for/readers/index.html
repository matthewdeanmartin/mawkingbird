For news readers and curious minds

Make room for the stories you want to read

Your news sources do not all live in one place. Mawkingbird brings RSS subscriptions, social feeds and saved articles into a browser client built for following a story beyond its headline.

Try the reading tools

Follow sources, not just the conversation

Add RSS feeds from the publications and independent writers you already read. Keep subscriptions together, then choose between scanning headlines and spending time with an article.

  • Import and export subscriptions with OPML, including feed folders.
  • Track read and unread items so you can return without starting over.
  • Read RSS alongside Mastodon and Bluesky posts in the same client.

Save the link. Come back to the article.

A good story often arrives when you have no time to read it. Bookmarks give those discoveries somewhere to wait, while a focused reader view makes supported pages easier to settle into.

  • Keep bookmarks and a reading library for later.
  • Fetch and format supported linked articles for a focused reading view.
  • Use reader preferences and Find to make longer pieces easier to navigate.

Let a feed have an ending

Browse feeds in pages instead of relying on an endless scroll. Catch up on the people and topics you chose, save what deserves more attention, and leave the next batch for later.

  • Use lists, tags and saved searches to organize social reading.
  • Follow your own selection of sources rather than one publication directory.
  • Keep the discussion and the longer read within reach.

Make it your own

Open the app preview, then visit RSS to add a feed or import your OPML subscriptions. Save an interesting link and try its reader view when extraction is available.

Article extraction depends on the source site and available proxy access. Mawkingbird does not bypass paywalls or guarantee that every page can be fetched.
