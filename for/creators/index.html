For writers, photographers and content creators

Keep the idea, shape the post, share the work

A caption, a photograph and a longer piece of writing do not all need the same workspace. Mawkingbird gives you social composing, saved drafts and a focused writing surface, with supported publication connections when configured.

Open your creative workspace

Let an unfinished idea stay unfinished

Save a draft instead of rushing to publish. Return to your words when they are ready, and use the writing workspace’s focused mode when you want more room to think.

  • Compose social posts and return to saved drafts.
  • Use a focused writing mode and review text before posting.
  • Work with supported thread tools when one post is not enough.

Publish photographs with more than a caption

Add images to supported social posts and write alt text to describe the photograph’s important details. Keep the story around the picture accessible, then use the image viewer to see how photos appear in the feed.

  • Attach photographs to supported Mastodon and Bluesky posts.
  • Add image descriptions rather than relying on the caption alone.
  • Use Bluesky’s supported image compression and multi-image posting tools.

Connect the publication tools you use

For work beyond a social post, supported publishing connections include Blogger and Hugo. Configure the services you need so a finished piece has a destination beyond the timeline.

  • Use available writing and publication tools when enabled.
  • Configure supported third-party publishing connections.
  • Keep research close with RSS, bookmarks and readable articles.

Make it your own

Explore the app, connect a supported social account, and save a first draft. For photography, try an image post with alt text. Set up a publishing connection when your longer work needs one.

Publishing requires the relevant account and service configuration. Some writing tools depend on deployment settings. Posting and media limits follow the connected network’s rules.
