For people leaving Twitter / X

Find your next conversation without starting from an empty feed

Moving on from Twitter or X is easier when you can see what another community feels like. Mawkingbird lets you explore a working preview, discover people and build a feed on Mastodon or Bluesky at your own pace.

Explore your next feed

Try the experience before choosing an account

Open the app and browse the preview before deciding where to sign in. The welcome flow helps you choose Mastodon, Bluesky or anonymous browsing, with the running app behind it.

  • Browse a sample feed before committing to a network.
  • Connect an existing Mastodon or Bluesky account when you are ready.
  • Use familiar replies, favourites or likes, and boosts or reposts.

Give your new timeline a starting point

The hardest part of a new network can be finding people. Starter collections and account search offer a practical beginning, while tags and lists help turn initial discoveries into a feed that feels personal.

  • Explore starter collections instead of guessing your first follows.
  • Search for people and inspect profiles before following.
  • Organize supported feeds with lists, topics and saved searches.

Choose what comes with you

A fresh start does not mean all your interests must move to one network. Keep independent news and blogs in RSS, save worthwhile posts, and switch between connected accounts as your communities grow.

  • Bring RSS subscriptions into the same reading routine.
  • Use bookmarks and reading tools for links worth keeping.
  • Manage supported follows, mutes and blocks as your feed develops.

Make it your own

Start with the preview and browse a starter collection. Choose Mastodon or Bluesky, connect or create an account with that network, then follow a few people whose posts you want to see again.

Mawkingbird does not automatically transfer your Twitter/X followers, posts or identity. Its optional Twitter connector needs separate configuration and API access; you do not need it to explore Mastodon or Bluesky.
