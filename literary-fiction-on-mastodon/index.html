Questions

Do I need an account?

Browse and preview public posts without an account. Sign in with your Mastodon account to follow your selection or create a list.

What belongs in this community?

Literary novels and short fiction, from contemporary realism and translated literature to experimental prose and magical realism. Reading, reviewing, editing, translating and writing fiction all count. Genre boundaries can overlap; recurring discussion of literary fiction matters more than a label. Poetry, nonfiction or general writing advice alone do not qualify. Hashtags are open conversations, so preview their posts before choosing.

How do I get onto this list?

Share literary-fiction content regularly and allow your server to suggest your profile. Substantive updates about your own fiction count too; a bio keyword alone does not. For help or removal, contact @mawkingbird@mastodon.social.

How we choose

People are listed when they asked their server to make them discoverable, or when they are public figures with public evidence: a Wikipedia or Wikidata entry, a YouTube channel with 10,000 subscribers, or 2,500 followers on Mastodon.

An account that looks like it wants to stay out of view is left out: few followers, hidden follower lists, no profile picture, together with a request not to be indexed. Anyone who turns off discoverability is never listed.

A language model checks that each person really posts about the topic, quoting their own posts as evidence. Every account is checked again each month: quiet accounts drop off and come back when they post again.

Need help?

Questions, or listed here and would rather not be? Message us on Mastodon and you will be removed for good. @mawkingbird@mastodon.social
