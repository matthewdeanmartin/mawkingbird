Space and astronomy on Mastodon

Mastodon is full of people looking up: professional astronomers and planetary scientists, astrophotographers with backyard telescopes, launch watchers, observatories, and science writers who explain what the latest mission found.

Starting from an empty timeline is slow. This page gives you a head start: the hashtags people actually use, the people worth following, and a server where the space community gathers.

Sign in with Mastodon to follow these hashtags and people in one go, or open the preview to read the feed first.

Questions

Do I need an account to read this?

No. Every hashtag here opens in Mawkingbird without an account. To follow people and tags you need a Mastodon account on any server; mastodon.social is a fine first choice, and you can move later and keep your followers.

Is Mastodon a good place for space and astronomy?

Yes. Many astronomers and space scientists moved to Mastodon, astrophotographers share their nights under #Astrophotography, and launches, eclipses and new telescope images get busy, knowledgeable threads. Conversations are slower than on Twitter and less driven by outrage.

How do I get onto this list?

Post about space or astronomy, use the hashtags above, and let your profile be suggested to others (your server’s Privacy and reach settings). The list is refreshed every month.

How we choose

People are listed when they asked their server to make them discoverable, or when they are public figures with public evidence: a Wikipedia or Wikidata entry, a YouTube channel with 10,000 subscribers, or 2,500 followers on Mastodon.

An account that looks like it wants to stay out of view is left out: few followers, hidden follower lists, no profile picture, together with a request not to be indexed. Anyone who turns off discoverability is never listed.

A language model checks that each person really posts about the topic, quoting their own posts as evidence. Every account is checked again each month: quiet accounts drop off and come back when they post again.

Need help?

Questions, or listed here and would rather not be? Message us on Mastodon and you will be removed for good. @mawkingbird@mastodon.social
